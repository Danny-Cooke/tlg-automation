On successful completion you will have the knowledge and practical skills to safely and confidently perform professional gel polish nail treatments. You will understand the anatomy and physiology of the natural nail and surrounding skin, carry out effective client consultations, identify contraindications and contra-actions, and maintain high standards of hygiene and health and safety. You will correctly prepare the natural nail, safely apply, cure, maintain and remove gel polish products, and give appropriate aftercare advice, with a sound understanding of the safe use, maintenance and storage of products and UV/LED curing lamps.

Course details

Course title
Gel Polish Nails CPD Course
Delivery
Classroom course at The Looking Glass Beauty School, 4a Roushill, Shrewsbury, with pre-course reading and support materials.
Duration
15 hours. Pre-course reading and knowledge activities, 7.5 guided learning hours on the taught day, and about 6 hours for three post-course case studies.
Entry requirements
Aged 18 or over, with a Level 2 Beauty Therapy, Nail Services or equivalent qualification that includes manicure and pedicure knowledge, nail structure and safe salon practice. Equivalent prior learning may be accepted subject to provider and insurer approval.
Assessment
A written theory assessment of 38 questions with an 80% pass mark, a separate practical assessment by direct observation on a suitable adult model (at least 80% overall and all safety-critical criteria met) and three satisfactory post-course case studies. A certificate is only issued once all three are complete; attendance alone is not sufficient.
Certificate
A course certificate from The Looking Glass Beauty School. This is a Continuing Professional Development course, not a regulated qualification.
Tutor
Jessica Jane Preston: VTCT Level 2 NVQ Diploma in Nail Services, Award in Education and Training, fourteen years in the industry, teaching since 2021.

What you will learn

  • Understand the anatomy and physiology of the natural nail and surrounding skin.
  • Carry out an effective client consultation and identify contraindications and contra-actions.
  • Maintain high standards of hygiene, health and safety throughout the service.
  • Correctly prepare the natural nail for gel polish.
  • Safely apply, cure, maintain and remove gel polish products.
  • Understand the safe use, maintenance and storage of products and UV/LED curing lamps.
  • Provide appropriate aftercare advice and deliver the service to an industry standard in a safe, hygienic and commercially acceptable manner.

Syllabus

  1. Anatomy and physiology of the nail unit. Structures, growth, what to protect.
  2. Contraindications, nail conditions and contra-actions. Recognise, adapt, postpone or refer; never diagnose.
  3. Consultation and consent. Consultation form, treatment plan, record keeping, data protection.
  4. Products, chemistry and curing. Base, colour and top coats, photoinitiators, lamp compatibility, the inhibition layer, allergy prevention.
  5. Health, safety and hygiene. COSHH, cleaning and disinfection, ventilation, UK legislation.
  6. Natural nail preparation. Shaping, cuticle work, de-shining, dehydrating.
  7. Application, cure and finish. Thin coats, capping the free edge, curing times, cleansing.
  8. Maintenance and removal. Soak-off removal without damage, troubleshooting lifting and chipping.
  9. Aftercare. Advice, the written handout, follow-up.
  10. Assessment. Written theory paper of 38 questions (pass 80%), observed practical on a model, three case studies.
