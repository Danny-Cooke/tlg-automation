A practical course for existing nail professionals who want to offer liquid-and-powder acrylic enhancements. You will understand the chemistry of monomer liquid and polymer powder, the mix ratio and how the bead sets; prepare the natural nail; fit tips and forms; build a balanced enhancement with a correctly placed apex using the three-zone method; infill, rebalance and remove safely; and work within the health and safety controls that acrylic systems demand, including ventilation, dust extraction and allergy prevention. Consultation, UK legislation, aftercare and honest advertising are covered throughout.

What you will learn

  • Explain the chemistry of liquid-and-powder acrylic, the mix ratio and how the product sets, and compare it with gel systems.
  • Identify the structures of the nail unit and recognise conditions that require the service to be adapted, postponed or refused.
  • Conduct and record a professional consultation, obtain informed consent and create an individual treatment plan.
  • Control monomer vapour, dust and skin contact in line with COSHH, the product's safety data sheet and current UK requirements.
  • Prepare the natural nail and fit tips and forms correctly.
  • Build a balanced acrylic overlay or extension with a correctly placed apex using the three-zone method.
  • Infill, rebalance and remove acrylic safely without damage to the natural nail, and provide appropriate aftercare.

Syllabus

  1. Acrylic systems and professional scope. What liquid-and-powder acrylic is, who it suits, comparison with gel systems.
  2. Nail anatomy and conditions. Structures to protect; recognise, adapt, postpone or refer.
  3. Consultation and consent. Consultation form, treatment plan, records.
  4. Chemistry, mix ratio and the bead. Monomer and polymer, primers, set time, working temperature.
  5. Health and safety for acrylic work. Vapour and dust control, extraction, PPE, allergy prevention, UK legislation.
  6. Preparation, tips and forms. Natural nail preparation, fitting and blending tips, fitting forms.
  7. Application: the three zones. Free edge, apex and stress area, cuticle zone; shaping and finishing.
  8. Infill, rebalance and removal. Reducing the old apex, rebuilding, soak-off removal after reducing bulk.
  9. Aftercare and troubleshooting. Advice, follow-up, reading a service that has gone wrong.
  10. Assessment. Theory paper, observed practical on a model, post-course case studies.
