The Looking Glass Beauty School teaches small-group, classroom-based nail and beauty courses in our training room at 4a Roushill, Shrewsbury, written and taught by Jess Preston. Each course is a Continuing Professional Development (CPD) course for qualified professionals, not a regulated qualification. Builder Gel Overlay and Short Extension CPD Course

Add builder gel overlays and short extensions to your services: nail anatomy and the conditions that mean adapt, postpone or refuse; how gel cures and how to control the heat spike; preparation, a balanced overlay with a correctly placed apex, short extensions on a tip or form, infills and safe removal.

Delivery: one taught day in the salon training room, with pre-course study.
Hours: 17.5 in total.
Entry: Level 2 Beauty Therapy, Nail Services or equivalent; aged 18 or over.
Assessment: theory paper, observed practical, three case studies.

Electric File (E-File) Nail Technique CPD Course

Safe, controlled use of an electric file on the natural nail and on the coatings and enhancements you already provide: the machine, the bits and grits, friction heat and how to prevent it, natural-nail preparation with a safety bit, reducing and removing gel polish, builder gel, hard gel and acrylic without reaching the plate, shaping and finishing, dust and vibration control.

Delivery: one taught day in the salon training room, with pre-course reading.
Hours: 15 in total.
Entry: Level 2 Beauty Therapy, Nail Services or equivalent plus a qualification in at least one coating or enhancement system; aged 18 or over.
Assessment: theory paper, observed practical, three case studies.

Lash and Brow Tint and Wax CPD Course

Eyelash and eyebrow tinting and eyebrow shaping by waxing, tweezing and trimming: the anatomy of the eye area, how tint works and why it sensitises, the patch-test system, mapping the brow, tinting with the eyes protected and closed, hot and strip wax, aftercare and what to do when something goes wrong.

Entry: Level 2 Beauty Therapy or equivalent · Hours: 15 (one taught day) · Assessment: theory paper, observed practical on a patch-tested model, three case studies
