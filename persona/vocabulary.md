# Vocabulary: the words The Looking Glass uses with clients

A word bank taken from the team's own Messenger replies (924 human replies,
February to October 2026). Counts are how many replies contained the phrase.
Use these words; avoid the ones at the bottom. The bot reads this file after
`jess.md` and `voice-guide.md`.
