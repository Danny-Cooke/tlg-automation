## Four tasks to complete before the taught day

Bring your answers with you. They are checked at registration and count as your 2.5 hours of pre-course study together with the lessons above.

1. **Read Chapters 1 to 10 of the manual** and note three questions to bring with you.
2. **Get the Safety Data Sheet** for the builder gel you will use. Write down the product name, its removal category (soak-off, file-off or combination) and its stated extension capability.
3. **Find the make, model and wattage of the lamp** you will use, and its cure schedule for this product.
4. **Write, in your own words, what a heat spike is** and exactly what you would do if a client reported one.

## Your model

You bring one suitable adult model for the afternoon. They must have healthy natural fingernails and no contraindication from lesson 3, and they will complete the consultation form on the day. If you cannot bring a model, tell the school at least seven days before the course.

## What to bring

Your pre-course answers, your own builder gel system and compatible lamp if you have them (the school supplies a system otherwise), closed shoes, hair tied back, no rings. Lunch is not provided; there is a 30-minute break.

## After the day

Three complete services on three different people within eight weeks, each recorded on Form D with consultation, treatment record, photographs with consent, aftercare and your own reflection. Educator feedback on each. Then your certificate.
