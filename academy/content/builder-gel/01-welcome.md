## Welcome

This is the online home of the **Builder Gel Overlay and Short Extension CPD Course**. The course itself is a taught day in the training room at The Looking Glass Beauty School in Shrewsbury. What you find here is everything around that day: the pre-course study, the learner manual, the support videos, and the forms you will use for your case studies.

## What the course covers

Builder gel overlays and short extensions on healthy natural fingernails. You will revisit nail anatomy and the conditions that mean a service must be adapted, postponed or refused; understand how gel cures and why builder gel carries a heat-spike risk that gel polish does not; and learn to prepare the natural nail, apply a slip layer, build a balanced overlay with a correctly placed apex, create a short extension on a tip or form, and carry out infills and safe removal.

## Scope boundary

This course does not teach gel polish, hard gel or liquid-and-powder acrylic, long extensions, or electric-file work on the natural nail. Those systems are compared so you can recognise them and work within scope; they need their own training.

## How you are assessed

1. A written theory paper of 50 marks, pass mark 80%.
2. A directly observed practical on a suitable adult model: at least 80% overall and every safety-critical criterion met.
3. Three complete services after the taught day, each recorded on the case-study forms.

Your CPD certificate is issued only when all three are satisfactory. Attendance alone is not enough.
