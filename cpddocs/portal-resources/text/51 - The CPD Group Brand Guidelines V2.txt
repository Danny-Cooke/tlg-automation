Brand Guidelines
These guidelines are for customers that have been provided with any
logo(s) by The CPD Group. The Brand Guidelines cover the use of logos on: 
• Documentation such as Letterheads, Business Cards and other avenues of
formatting. 
• Marketing and Advertising Activities such as Websites and Social Media •
Certificates. 
• Branding and promotion of Registered Activities. 
• Branding and promotion of Provider Activities. 
*Branding guidelines are not solely limited to just the above avenues* If you
do have any queries, please contact: info@thecpd.group
Why is this guide important?
Who is this guide for?
These guidelines outline the general rules when using The CPD Group brand
assets. You may only use the approved brand assets that are provided to you by
The CPD Group. Any other logos or images are not approved for use.

Brand Guidelines
The CPD Group Rosette Logo 
How Can I Use The CPD Group Logo?
For representing your affiliation with The CPD Group only. When your free
Provider approval application has been processed and awarded, you will
be granted a non-exclusive licence to use The CPD Group logo on your
Promotional Materials, Website and Social Media Platforms.
The CPD Group
info@thecpd.group
0300 3732 528
Where The CPD Group logo CAN be used: 
Any marketing or promotional materials that are used to promote
your organisation only, such as social media, website homepage,
letterheads, business cards etc. 
Where The CPD Group logo CANNOT be used: 
Anything relating to a CPD Training Course or CPD Activity i.e
Certificates, Activity Promotion, Activity Listing on Website
Using this logo in a way that is not permitted by The CPD Group,
as defined by this Brand Guidelines document, may result in your
account being suspended and your accreditation being
withdrawn. 
NOTE: You must NOT use this, The CPD Group logo, to imply that
your CPD Training Courses or CPD Activities are accredited by us.

How can I use my accreditedactivity logo?
For the accreditation of a single activity only. 
Once your activity is accredited with The CPD Group, you will receive a
unique activity logo with your verifiable accreditation number.
The CPD Group

0300 3732 528
Where your Activity logo CAN be used: 
Where your Provider logo CANNOT be used: 
Any generic marketing that does not refer to the specific activity

The CPD Register.
