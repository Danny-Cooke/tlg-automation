PROTECTING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION
Your personal data is protected under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can find further information about your rights and the role of the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/.
[Company Name] is registered as a data controller and is required to take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. This includes making regular backups of our systems and data, maintaining appropriate security measures, and ensuring that all parties with access to your data are subject to confidentiality obligations.
Where applicable, your account with us is password protected. All information is stored on a secure server, accessible only to a limited number of authorised staff. If you believe someone else has access to your password or account, please contact us immediately and change your password without delay.
Please be aware that while we take all appropriate steps to protect your information, data transmission over the internet cannot always be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You use our website at your own risk.
