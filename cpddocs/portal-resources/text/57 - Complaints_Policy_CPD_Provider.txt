COMPLAINTS POLICY

Complaint — a grievance, problem, difficulty, or concern raised by a learner, client, or other stakeholder regarding the services or conduct of [Company Name].
Policy Statement
[Company Name] recognises the importance of complaints as a valuable source of feedback about the quality of our CPD training and associated services. We are committed to handling all complaints promptly, fairly, and with respect, and to using the information we receive to drive continuous improvement.
A complaint is a way of letting us know that you are not satisfied with a particular aspect of our service. We welcome your feedback and encourage you to raise any concerns without fear that doing so will affect the service you receive.
A complaint may relate to:
A delay or lack of response from our team
Discourtesy or unprofessional conduct by a member of staff or trainer
A failure to deliver what was agreed or expected
The standard, content, or delivery of a CPD training programme
Any other aspect of the service we provide
Anonymous Complaints
We understand that some individuals may feel uncomfortable raising a complaint in their own name. All complaints are treated in the strictest confidence and will not result in any detriment to the complainant. If, however, a complaint is submitted anonymously and no contact details are provided, we may be unable to respond with the outcome of any investigation.
Complaints Procedure
Stage 1 — Informal Resolution
In the first instance, we encourage the complainant to raise the matter directly with the member of staff or trainer concerned. Many concerns can be resolved quickly at this stage. Where a resolution is reached informally, the matter should be documented using Appendix 1 (Record of Complaint) and submitted to the Administrator for filing by the end of the next working day. No further action will be required.
Where this is not possible or appropriate, or where the complainant does not feel able to raise the matter directly, they should proceed to Stage 2.
Stage 2 — Formal Complaint
If informal resolution is not possible or has not been successful, the complainant should submit a formal written complaint to the Training Manager within 48 hours of the incident or concern arising. The complaint should be documented using Appendix 1 and submitted to the Administrator.
On receipt of the formal complaint, the Training Manager will:
Acknowledge receipt of the complaint within 2 working days
Bring the nature of the complaint to the attention of the relevant staff member within 48 hours of receipt
Investigate the matter fairly and impartially
Contact the complainant with a view to reaching a resolution
Where a resolution cannot be reached through correspondence, the Training Manager will arrange a meeting with all relevant parties within 30 days of the complaint being received. The outcome of this meeting will be recorded and communicated in writing to the complainant. This decision will be final at this stage.
Stage 3 — Escalation
Where a complainant remains dissatisfied following the formal complaint process, they may refer the matter to a senior manager or director of [Company Name] for a final review. The outcome of this review will be communicated in writing.
Where a complaint relates to an assessment decision or programme outcome, the complainant should also refer to the separate Appeals Policy.
Record Keeping and Confidentiality
The Administrator will maintain a secure record of all complaints received, together with the actions taken and outcomes reached. These records will be made available on request to senior management for the purposes of quality review and continuous improvement.
All complaints must be treated as strictly confidential and discussed only with those directly involved in the investigation and resolution process. Complaint records will be retained in accordance with our data retention policy and in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed annually, or sooner if there are significant changes to our services, staffing, or relevant legislation. All revisions will be documented and communicated to relevant staff.
