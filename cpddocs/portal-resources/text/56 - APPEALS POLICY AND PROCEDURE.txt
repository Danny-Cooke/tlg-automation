APPEALS POLICY AND PROCEDURE
For CPD Training Providers
The Appeals Policy is designed to protect the interests of all learners and also to protect the integrity of CPD training provision.
Learners have the right to appeal in the event that they are dissatisfied with the following:
The assessment decisions made by the Training Provider regarding CPD course completion or competency
The quality of training provision delivered by the Training Provider
Any decisions made by the Training Provider regarding CPD certification or accreditation status
A copy of the appeals procedure is available to all learners upon request.
There is an informal and formal procedure available. The formal procedure is only to be followed if the informal procedure has failed or is inappropriate for the circumstances.
Every attempt will be made to resolve disputes as near as possible to the point of origin. The Training Provider will keep appeals records for inspection for a minimum of 18 months.
Informal Procedure
Where a learner wishes to make an appeal against the quality of provision or assessment decisions made by the Training Provider, they should first attempt to resolve the matter by a direct approach to the Course Trainer/Tutor.
If the matter remains unresolved, the learner may request a personal meeting with the Training Provider Manager/Director.
Before the personal meeting, the Training Provider Manager/Director should obtain an independent second opinion on the initial decision from another qualified member of the training team or an external subject matter expert where appropriate.
If, after any action taken to resolve the dispute by the Training Provider Manager/Director, the matter is not satisfactorily resolved, the learner may use the formal procedure.
Formal Procedure
Once the informal procedure has been exhausted, or if it is inappropriate to the circumstances, the formal procedure is to be followed.
The learner will be required to submit a formal complaint in writing to the Training Provider Manager/Director. This should include: 
Details of the appeal
The grounds for the appeal
Any supporting evidence
The outcome the learner is seeking
Within 10 working days of receiving the written appeal, the Training Provider Manager/Director will: 
Acknowledge receipt of the appeal
Conduct a thorough investigation
Review all relevant evidence and documentation
Communicate the decision to the learner in writing
The decision will include: 
A clear explanation of the outcome
The reasons for the decision
Details of any actions to be taken
Information about further appeal options if applicable
If the Training Provider's CPD courses are accredited by The CPD Group or another CPD accreditation body, and the appeal relates to CPD accreditation matters, learners may contact the relevant accreditation body for guidance. However, the Training Provider must first complete their internal appeals procedure.
Further Appeals
Any learner wishing to appeal against the operation of the Appeals Procedure itself can do so in writing to the Training Provider Manager/Director.
If the learner remains dissatisfied following the completion of the formal appeals procedure, they may:
Contact the relevant CPD accreditation body (if applicable)
Seek guidance from relevant professional bodies or trade associations
Pursue independent mediation or alternative dispute resolution
Record Keeping
The Training Provider will maintain comprehensive records of all appeals, including:
Written complaints and supporting documentation
Investigation notes and findings
Decisions and outcomes
Actions taken
Correspondence with the learner
All records will be stored securely and retained for a minimum of 18 months in accordance with data protection requirements.
