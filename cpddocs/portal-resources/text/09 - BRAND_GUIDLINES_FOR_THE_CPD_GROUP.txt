Why is this guide important?
These guidelines outline the general rules when using The CPD Group brand
assets. You may only use the approved brand assets that are provided to
you by The CPD Group.
Any other logos or images are not approved for use.
Who is this guide for?
These guidelines are for customers that have been provided with any logo(s)
by The CPD Group.
The Brand Guidelines cover the use of logos on:
• Documentation such as Letterheads, Business Cards and other avenues of
formatting.
• Marketing and Advertising Activities such as Websites and Social Media
• Certificates.
• Branding and promotion of Registered Activities.
• Branding and promotion of Provider Activities.
*Branding guidelines are not solely limited to just the
above avenues*
If you do have any queries, please contact: info@thecpd.group

GUIDELINESBrand
The CPD Group Logo
For representing your affiliation with The CPD Group
only.
When your free Provider Accreditation application
has been processed and awarded, you will be
granted a non-exclusive licence to use The CPD
Group logo on your Promotional Materials, Website
and Social Media Platforms. 
            Where The CPD Group logo CAN be used: Any
marketing or promotional materials that are used to
promote your organisation only, such as social
media, website homepage, letterheads, business
cards etc.
            Where The CPD Group logo CANNOT be used:
Anything relating to a CPD Training Course or CPD
Activity i.e Certificates, Activity Promotion, Activity
Listing on Website.
Using this logo in a way that is not permitted by The
CPD Group, as defined by this Brand Guidelines
document, may result in your account being
suspended and your accreditation being withdrawn.
NOTE: You must NOT use this, The CPD Group logo,
to imply that your CPD Training Courses or CPD
Activities are Accredited by us.
THE CPD GROUP Logos
04

When becoming an Accredited Provider with The
CPD Group, you will receive a unique logo with your
provider number.
      Where the Provider logo CAN be used: Any
marketing materials such as social media, website
homepage, letterheads, business cards etc.
             Where the Provider logo CANNOT be used:
Anything relating to an Activity i.e Certificates,
Activity Promotion, Activity Listing on Website.
The logo should be linked to your provider profile on
The CPD Register.
Being an Accredited Provider does not mean that
your activities are Accredited.
Falsifying logos may result in your account being
suspended and your accreditation being withdrawn.
NOTE: You must NOT use this, the Accredited
Provider logo, to imply that your CPD Training
Courses or CPD Activities are Accredited by us.

THE CPD GROUP Logos
05
Once your activity is accredited with The CPD Group,
you will receive a unique activity logo with
accreditation number and expiry date.
            CAN Anything relating to the Activity the logo is
accredited for i.e Certificates, Activity Promotion,
Activity Listing on Website.
      CANNOT Any generic marketing the specific               
activity such as social media, website homepage,
letterheads, business cards etc.
Each activity logo should be linked to the
corresponding activity on The CPD Register.
Activities are only accredited once they have a
unique logo and number and are listed on The CPD
Register.
Having one activity accredited by The CPD Group
does not mean all other Activities you have are
accredited too.
Falsifying logos may result in your account being
suspended and your accreditation being withdrawn.
Activity Logo
For the Accreditation of a single activity only.
