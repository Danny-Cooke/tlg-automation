## What it is

### CPD podcast accreditation

 CPD podcast accreditation is the formal assessment and approval of a podcast episode as a Continuing Professional Development activity. The CPD Group offers dedicated podcast accreditation, recognising that podcasts have become a significant and valuable medium for professional learning.

 When a podcast episode is CPD accredited by The CPD Group, it confirms that the content delivers genuine professional development value. Listeners can earn CPD credits by listening, and can download a verifiable CPD certificate as evidence of their learning — allowing them to log the time towards their professional body's annual CPD requirements.

## The model

### What is CPD podcast accreditation?

 Podcast accreditation from The CPD Group involves an individual assessment of your podcast episode to confirm it has defined educational content, a clear professional audience, an appropriate duration, and delivers genuine learning value that listeners can apply in their professional role.

 Accredited podcast episodes receive a unique QR code. Listeners scan the QR code — displayed in your show notes, episode page, or social media posts — to access their personalised CPD certificate. This certificate confirms the episode title, the number of CPD credits earned, and The CPD Group's verifiable accreditation number, which can be checked on The CPD Register.

## Why accredit

### The benefits of CPD podcast accreditation

#### Makes your content count towards listeners' CPD

 Many professionals need to log a set number of CPD hours each year to maintain their professional registration or membership. By having your podcast episodes CPD accredited, you enable listeners to count the time they spend engaging with your content as part of their professional development record. This significantly increases the practical value of your podcast to professional audiences.

#### Increases audience reach and discoverability

 Accredited podcast episodes are listed on The CPD Register, where professionals and employers actively search for quality-assured CPD activities. Displaying The CPD Group's accreditation logo in your podcast artwork, show notes, and promotional materials signals quality assurance and attracts listeners who are actively seeking accredited learning.

#### Differentiates your podcast in a crowded market

 Podcast accreditation is still relatively uncommon in most professional sectors, which means accredited content stands out clearly from the vast majority of unaccredited podcasts. For content creators targeting audiences in regulated industries — healthcare, law, financial services, engineering — CPD accreditation is a meaningful signal of professional credibility.

#### Generates listener data

 When listeners scan your QR code to receive their CPD certificate, The CPD Group provides data on engagement with each accredited episode. This gives podcast producers valuable insight into listener behaviour and the reach of their professional content.

## FAQs

### About podcast accreditation

 Does every episode of a podcast need to be accredited separately?

Yes. Each episode submitted for accreditation is assessed individually, as each episode contains different content. This individual assessment model ensures that every accredited episode genuinely meets CPD standards — it is the same approach The CPD Group takes with courses and webinars.

 Can older podcast episodes be submitted for accreditation?

Yes. There is no requirement for episodes to be new at the point of submission. If you have existing episodes that deliver professional learning value, they can be submitted for assessment.

 What length does a podcast episode need to be?

There is no strict minimum length, but an episode needs to be long enough to deliver meaningful professional learning. As a general rule, one CPD credit is equivalent to one hour of learning. Shorter episodes may earn fractional credits. The CPD Group's team will advise on the appropriate credit allocation during the assessment process.
