## What it is

### CPD trainer accreditation

 CPD trainer accreditation is the individual assessment and formal recognition of an individual trainer, coach, consultant, or subject matter expert as a credentialed CPD professional. The CPD Group's trainer accreditation confirms that an individual has the expertise, qualifications, and professional capability to deliver high-quality CPD-accredited learning.

 Unlike provider accreditation — which recognises an organisation — trainer accreditation recognises the individual. This makes it particularly valuable for freelance trainers, independent coaches, consultants, and subject matter experts who deliver learning across multiple organisations, platforms, or contexts. Your accreditation travels with you, not with the company you happen to be working for at the time.

## The credential

### What is CPD trainer accreditation?

 When The CPD Group assesses and accredits an individual trainer, it confirms that the trainer's credentials, professional experience, and subject matter expertise meet the standards required to deliver CPD-accredited learning. The assessment takes into account the trainer's qualifications, professional background, areas of expertise, and track record in delivering professional development.

 Accredited trainers receive a personal CPD trainer accreditation credential from The CPD Group — a recognised, verifiable mark of professional standing that can be displayed on their website, LinkedIn profile, marketing materials, and course pages.

## Why apply

### The benefits of CPD trainer accreditation

#### Portable professional credential

 As a freelance trainer or independent professional, your reputation is built on your personal credentials rather than the organisation you work for. CPD trainer accreditation from The CPD Group provides an individually verified mark of professional standing that you can present to clients and delegates regardless of the context in which you are delivering.

#### Increased client confidence

 When potential clients are evaluating trainers, an individually assessed CPD accreditation is a meaningful differentiator. It confirms that your expertise and delivery capability have been assessed by The CPD Group — not just self-declared. In a competitive market for training and coaching services, this assessment can be a deciding factor in winning new business.

#### Access to course and activity accreditation

 Accredited trainers can apply to have their individual courses, workshops, and other learning activities accredited by The CPD Group. Having both personal trainer accreditation and activity-level accreditation provides the strongest possible signal of quality to professional audiences.

#### Listing on The CPD Register

 Accredited trainers are listed on The CPD Register, a public directory of CPD professionals and providers. Professionals and employers who are looking for CPD-accredited training and coaching actively search The CPD Register, increasing your discoverability to the audiences most likely to engage your services.

#### Part of a credentialed professional community

 Trainer accreditation from The CPD Group connects you to a community of over 13,000 approved providers and accredited professionals. This community spans every major industry and sector, reflecting the breadth of professional development work that The CPD Group supports.

## The assessment

### What does the trainer accreditation assessment involve?

The CPD Group's trainer assessment evaluates the individual against a professional framework covering:

#### Qualifications

Formal educational or professional qualifications relevant to the subject area.

#### Professional experience

Years and depth of experience in the relevant field.

#### Subject matter expertise

Depth of knowledge in the area being taught.

#### Delivery experience

Track record in delivering training, coaching, or facilitation.

#### Professional standing

Industry recognition, memberships, or professional body status where relevant.

## Which do I need?

### Trainer accreditation vs CPD provider accreditation

Provider accreditation recognises an organisation. Trainer accreditation recognises an individual.

#### Provider accreditation

If you run a training business and deliver courses through that business, you need provider accreditation for the organisation.

 Become a CPD Provider

#### Trainer accreditation

If you deliver training independently — as a freelancer, coach, or consultant — trainer accreditation gives you a personal credential that you can present to clients.

 Apply for Trainer Accreditation

#### Many professionals hold both

 If you run a small training business but also deliver some work independently, having both provider and trainer accreditation gives you maximum credibility across all the contexts in which you work. The CPD Group's team can advise on the right combination for your specific situation.
