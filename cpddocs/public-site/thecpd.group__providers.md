## Got any questions?

We're here to answer them!

###
 What is a CPD Provider?

 When we use the term 'Approved CPD Provider', we're referring to a company or organisation
 that possesses the credibility and expertise to deliver effective CPD learning.

###
 What are the benefits of becoming a CPD Provider?

 Our FREE CPD Provider Approval service assesses your company to ensure that you are CPD
 ready and meet our required standards. Once we've assessed your organisation, you'll receive
 a unique provider approval logo, certificate, and approval number that you can use to
 display your company's CPD Provider status. By becoming a CPD Provider with us, you're
 demonstrating to your customers that you're fully equipped to deliver high quality learning,
 and that they can trust the service you will deliver.

###
 What will I receive?

 Not only will your organisation feature on The CPD Register free of charge, but you will
 also be issued with a unique approval number and logo as well as your very own CPD Provider
 certificate to proudly display in your establishment!

###
 What do I need to do?

 The application process is simple and paperless, saving you hassle whilst also doing your
 bit to save the planet. We'll need you to upload some details about your company, such as
 your company address, contact details and what your company does.
 We'll also need to know what policies you have in place to give us an insight into how your company operates. Once you've done
 this, submit your application and our team will aim to review it within a few working days.
 Simple!

###
 Is it really free?

 Yes! It really is free! There are no catches to having your company approved with us, we
 promise.

###
 Does CPD Approval cover my courses?

 It's important to note that CPD Approval does not accredit your courses or activities. Our
 accreditation service comes in two simple steps:
 Step
 1: Approving your business for free.
Step 2:
 Accrediting your individual activities for a small cost.

 flexible payment plans and our pricing is affordable and transparent!
