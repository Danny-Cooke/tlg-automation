## CPD Event Accreditation FAQs

Common questions about getting your conferences and events accredited by The CPD Group.

 Can conferences and events be CPD accredited?

Yes — The CPD Group accredits a wide range of events: conferences, summits, exhibitions, multi-speaker showcases, and CPD expos. Each event is assessed against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework, with separate credit values often assigned per session so delegates earn credits for what they actually attend.

 How are CPD credits calculated for multi-session events?

For multi-session events we typically accredit each session individually so delegates earn credits based on the sessions they attend. As a general rule, one CPD credit equals one hour of structured learning content. The total credit value across the event is confirmed during accreditation assessment.

 Can I issue CPD certificates to event attendees?

Yes. Once your event is accredited, you can issue personalised CPD certificates to attendees confirming the credits earned. Each certificate carries a unique accreditation number on The CPD Register that attendees and their employers can verify online.

 Do online and hybrid events qualify for CPD accreditation?

Yes — online, in-person, and hybrid events are all eligible. The CPD Group assesses based on learning quality, defined objectives, professional audience, and delivery — not the format. Most professional bodies now accept online CPD equally alongside in-person events.

 Can speakers at my event display CPD-accredited credentials?

Yes — individual speakers can pursue separate Trainer accreditation from The CPD Group, which they hold personally regardless of which event they're speaking at. This is particularly valuable for keynote speakers and industry experts who want to demonstrate their own CPD credibility to event organisers and audiences.
