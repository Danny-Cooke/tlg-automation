### The CPD Group: Member Update

Through our Member Report mechanism and subsequent investigation, we have withdrawn the accreditation for the following organisation:

Organisation Name: Lift Aesthetics Academy Ltd (T/A The Clinic Club)

Approved CPD Provider Number: 779042

Reason for CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal: Breach of The CPD Group's Terms and Conditions, under section 5. Intellectual Property & 6. Termination

CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal Date: 20th March 2024

Background:

The above organisation gained Approved CPD Provider status from The CPD Group on the 6th of May 2021.

Listed below are the CPD Training courses that Lift Aesthetics Academy Ltd (T/A The Clinic Club) had CPD Accredited by us:

·     1009766 - Foundation Dermal Fillers

·     1009767 - Russian Lips

·     1009768 - Advanced Dermal Fillers Course

·     1009770 - Foundation Anti Wrinkle

·     1009802 - IV Infusion Therapy Training.

·     1009815 - Skin Booster

·     1009858 - PRP Vampire facial

·     1010016 - Aqualyx Fat Dissolving

·     1010115 - COG/ PDO threads

·     1010565 - Micro-Needling

·     1010566 - Microdermabrasion

·     1010567 - Dermaplaning

·     1010568 - Express Facials

·     1010599 - Mild Peels

·     1010769 - LED Light Therapy

·     1012966 - Level 3 Anatomy & Physiology

**CPD Accreditation for all the above listed course titles has been revoked as of 20th March 2024 – Any courses delivered after the 20th March 2024 are NOT CPD Accredited by The CPD Group**

The above listed subjects are the ONLY CPD Training titles and subjects that have been CPD Accredited by The CPD Group. If any member of the public is in receipt of certification from Lift Aesthetics Academy Ltd (T/A The Clinic Club) that presents one of the above listed titles or subjects, and is dated prior to 20th March 2024, your certification holds valid CPD Accreditation and is not affected by this withdrawal of services.

However, if any member of the public is in receipt of certification from Lift Aesthetics Academy Ltd (T/A The Clinic Club) that presents an alternative course title or subject, or it is dated post 20th March 2024, you can be assured it does not hold CPD Accreditation from The CPD Group.

However, please be advised that this does not mean that the course was not Accredited as Lift Aesthetics Academy Ltd (T/A The Clinic Club) may have the specific course title or subject CPD Accredited by an alternative CPD Accreditation Body.

We would highly recommend contacting the Training Provider directly with your enquires.

Please Note: The CPD Group have never had a working relationship directly with The Clinic Club.

The CPD Group take all comments, feedback, complaints, concerns, queries good, bad or indifferent extremely seriously in order to ensure high standards of CPD and will fully investigate all reports thoroughly.
