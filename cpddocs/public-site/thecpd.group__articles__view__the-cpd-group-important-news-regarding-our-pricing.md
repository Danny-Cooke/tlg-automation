 The CPD Group: Important News Regarding Our Pricing

 Jess Hart

 November 29, 2023

 3 min read

 Share:

The CPD Group Pricing Update

At The CPD Group, our journey has always been defined by a commitment to excellence and a passion for raising CPD standards across the globe. Since being founded back in 2017, The CPD Group have demonstrated a dedication to customer value and experience, delivering a high-quality, affordable accreditation service that is consistently innovative and forward thinking. In the seven years since The CPD Group’s inception, we have rapidly grown into an internationally recognised business, working with 7,000+ organisations from around the world. In this time, we have maintained our prices at the same level to provide a service that is accessible to all.

As a result of the current economic climate and our rapid expansion, the time has now arrived to change this. From early 2024, we will be increasing the price of our activity and trainer accreditation services. This has not been an easy decision to make, but as the landscape of professional development evolves, so must we. This adjustment is not just about a change in pricing; it's a testament to our dedication to enhancing the value and quality of the services we provide. It's an investment in your success and the global standard of CPD.

Why are we increasing our prices?

From the outset, our mission has been clear: to raise CPD standards and empower professionals worldwide. This adjustment enables us to not only maintain but to enhance our commitment. By investing in The CPD Group, you're not merely purchasing a service; you're becoming part of a community that values excellence, innovation, and your continuous growth.

The price adjustment reflects our commitment to providing you with an even more robust and valuable accreditation experience. It allows us to invest in cutting-edge resources, advanced technologies, and a team of experts dedicated to your success. This is not just about a service; it's about your journey toward excellence.

Our service has always been more than just a transaction. It's a partnership that goes beyond accreditation. It’s an investment in the growth of your business. The CPD Group Team is here to guide you, support you, and ensure that your experience with us exceeds your expectations. This adjustment allows us to continue delivering on this promise.

What about our free services?

Don't worry, our CPD Provider approval, Event accreditation and Employer accreditation services will remain completely free of charge. Whilst we considered raising the prices of these services, upon discussion with our team and advisory board, we decided that these services should remain free. This is because we want to make our accreditation services as accessible as possible and these free services allow our customers to get to know our business and learn more about the accreditation processes before accrediting their CPD activities and/or Trainers.

A final thanks to you, our customers!

﻿

We want to express our gratitude for your continued trust and partnership. The CPD Group is not just an accreditation service; it's a community of professionals dedicated to excellence. Together, we are shaping the future of CPD standards worldwide.

Should you have any questions or thoughts regarding this adjustment, please don't hesitate to reach out. Your voice matters, and we're always here to listen!

Thank you,

The CPD Group Team
