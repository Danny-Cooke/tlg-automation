## CPD Webinar Accreditation FAQs

Common questions about getting your webinars accredited by The CPD Group.

 Can webinars be CPD accredited?

Yes — The CPD Group accredits both live and recorded webinars. Whether you run live virtual sessions, on-demand replays, or webinar series, each session can be assessed against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework provided it has defined learning objectives and a clear professional audience.

 How many CPD credits does a webinar earn?

One CPD credit typically equals one hour of structured learning. So a 60-minute webinar earns ~1 credit and a 90-minute webinar ~1.5 credits. The exact credit value is confirmed during accreditation assessment based on the actual learning content delivered.

 Do attendees receive a CPD certificate after a webinar?

Yes. Once your webinar is accredited, you can issue personalised CPD certificates to attendees on completion, confirming credits earned. Each certificate carries a unique accreditation number listed on The CPD Register, which delegates and employers can verify online.

 Can I accredit a recurring webinar series under one accreditation?

For a recurring webinar with the same content, one accreditation covers every delivery within the validity period. For a series with distinct topics per session, each session is typically accredited individually so attendees earn the appropriate credits for the specific session they attend.

 How long does webinar accreditation take?

The CPD Group typically begins reviewing webinar submissions within 2 to 3 weeks. Submissions with clear learning objectives, defined duration, complete trainer credentials, and a structured agenda move through assessment fastest. Contact us early if you have a specific launch date.
