 The Hub of CPD

 Emma Kynoch

 November 25, 2022

 3 min read

 Share:

Hello there! Welcome to thehub of CPD.

We’d like to take the opportunity to welcome you to The CPD Group Blog. Here, you’ll find interesting articles on professional development, business tips, information about our services and lots more. The CPD Blog is directed towards anyone interested in learning and development – including learners, CPD providers, trainers, and those who want to learn more about CPD as a whole.

Our blog is a great resource for businesses providing CPD learning, as well as individual learners taking part in Continuing Professional Development. We’ll be posting articles about what makes great CPD training, top tips for running your learning business, advice on how to make the most of your learning, how each of our accreditation services work, important announcements from us, and loads more good stuff! Keep an eye out so you don’t miss a thing!

At The CPD Group, we have over three decades of combined experience in the learning and development industry. Our goal is to create a space where you can find all the answers to your questions and discover valuable information that will help you along your CPD journey. Whether you’re a complete newbie to the world of CPD, or an established CPD business, there’s something for everyone here!

So, what are you waiting for? Sit back, relax and learn something new! Dive into our latest blog posts here.

Looking for CPD Accreditation?

We accredit a wide range of CPD activities, so take a look at the different types of accreditation we offer to find what suits you best:

- CPD Courses

- CPD Webinars

- CPD Edutorials

- CPD Audio Podcasts

- CPD Video Podcasts

- CPD Accredited Events

- CPD Trainers

- CPD Employers

Every activity we accredit is assessed individually against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework and listed on The CPD Register, where you can verify its accreditation status before you commit.

How to become an accredited CPD Provider

We hope this article was helpful. The CPD Group is the first CPD accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, working with over 13,000 approved providers across 170+ countries and 100+ industries. Every course, webinar, event or podcast we accredit is assessed on its own merit, never as part of a blanket approval.

If you're delivering training, running events, hosting a podcast, or creating any learning content that offers genuine professional development value, we'd love to help you get it accredited. Visit our Become a CPD Provider page or contact our team for a friendly chat about what we can do for you. If you're a learner looking to track, store and verify your own CPD, create your free CPD Passport - it connects directly to The CPD Register for instant verification of your accredited learning.
