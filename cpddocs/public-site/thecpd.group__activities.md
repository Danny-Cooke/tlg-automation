##
 Got a Question About Course Accreditation?

 Take a look at our frequently asked questions below.

###
 What do the accreditation fees cover?

 Your fees cover the cost of course accreditation. Our specialist
 accreditation compliance team carefully reviews every course that is
 submitted to us, verifying the content and closely mapping it against the
 CPD ACCREDITED Framework.
 This takes a lot of manpower, so we need to cover
 our costs!

###
 Are there any hidden fees?

 Nope! The prices you see are the prices you’ll pay. You can use our Course Cost Calculator to see what the fees will be for
 you based on the number of
 courses you want to have accredited, and the length of time you’d like the
 accreditation to last.

###
 What happens if my activity doesn't meet CPD standards?

 We will never simply refuse course accreditation. We’re people helping
 people and we are committed to helping our customers reach the highest CPD
 standards. Should our accreditation team feel that there are areas that
 require improvement, we will relay this feedback back to you and support you
 in implementing these amendments. Once you have made the necessary
 amendments simply resubmit your application and our team will reassess these
 changes against the ACCREDITED Framework.

###
 How do I submit an course for accreditation?

 We have made it easier (and faster!) than ever to get your Courses accredited. Simply head to your account, click into ‘Manage My Activities’, then ‘Manage My Courses’ and select your plan. Once we have secured your plan and preferred payment option, you can begin submitting your Courses - easy!

###
 How long does the assessment process take?

 For Course Accreditation, we generally aim to start reviewing your course
 within 2-3 weeks. Please note that turnaround times will depend on the
 length and type of activity. During peak periods, we allow up to 6 weeks for
 course accreditation.

###
 What happens if I make changes to my activity?

 We encourage customers to keep their Course Accreditation up-to-date in line
 with new techniques, research, technology etc and expect that you may need
 to make amendments to the course materials/resources throughout your
 commitment period. Updated Courses must be re-submitted and re-accredited
 but please be assured this will come at no additional cost to you. This is
 just part of our commitment to raising CPD standards across the world.
