 We're The CPD Awards Headline Sponsor

#
 We're The CPD Awards Headline Sponsor

 Emma Kynoch

 November 29, 2022

 3 min read

 Share:

The CPD Group are thrilled to announce that they are the headline sponsors for next year’s CPD Awards ceremony!

The CPD Group are excited to officially announce that they are the headline sponsors of The CPD Awards 2023! The CPD Awards is a ceremony hosted by The CPD Register that recognises excellent CPD learning across the board. The awards celebrate exemplary CPD in many forms, from innovative CPD activities to employers that value the CPD of their employees.

As one of the fastest growing accreditation bodies in the world, The CPD Group pride themselves on their dedication to raising CPD standards, working with thousands of CPD Providers, Trainers, and Employers from around the globe. Therefore, they are proud to be supporting an event that recognises individuals and organisations that demonstrate a commitment to exemplary CPD learning. The CPD Group’s Chief Operation Officer, Andy Donnachie, is thrilled to be a part of the awards:

“It is with pride we align ourselves with such a worthy celebration and look forward to seeing all of the wonderful nominees and winners pick up their prizes on the night, and at long last, being recognised for the fantastic work they do. It is also an excellent opportunity for our customers to showcase themselves with this extra accreditation.” – Andy Donnachie, COO

As well as sponsoring the event, The CPD Group will be presenting and sponsoring the CPD Provider of the Year award. This award will recognise a company that goes above and beyond to deliver excellent CPD learning. In total, there will be 12 awards presented on the evening, including CPD Trainer of the Year, Online Course of the Year, and the Positive Impact Award.

The event will be held in the historic venue of London Guildhall on Friday the 24th of November 2023. The awards will be judged by a panel of experts, and there will be special guest hosts and entertainment throughout the evening. It’s going to be a night to remember!

More information on The CPD Group sponsoring The CPD Awards can be found here.

If you have someone in mind to put forward for an award, you can submit nominations via The CPD Awards website.

How to become an accredited CPD Provider

We hope this article was helpful. The CPD Group is the first CPD accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, working with over 13,000 approved providers across 170+ countries and 100+ industries. Every course, webinar, event or podcast we accredit is assessed on its own merit, never as part of a blanket approval.

If you're delivering training, running events, hosting a podcast, or creating any learning content that offers genuine professional development value, we'd love to help you get it accredited. Visit our Become a CPD Provider page or contact our team for a friendly chat about what we can do for you.
