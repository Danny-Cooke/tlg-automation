#
 What is CPD?

##
 CPD Explained

###
 What is CPD?

 To understand what "CPD Accredited" means, we first have to understand what CPD is.

 CPD stands for Continuing Professional Development, and refers to any activity that encourages participants to learn new skills, knowledge or expertise that will help them progress in their chosen career.

 CPD can take a variety of forms. It includes, but is not limited to the following:

 CPD Courses

 CPD Webinars

 CPD Events and Conferences

 CPD Podcasts

 CPD Publications

 To learn more about the definition of CPD,

 check out this blog!

##
 Accreditation Explained

###
 What is CPD Accreditation?

 CPD Accreditation is a process where a third party, such as The CPD Group, will assess a CPD activity and make an expert judgment on whether it meets CPD standards. When assessing a CPD activity, the CPD accreditation body will use a certain set of criteria to ensure that every aspect of the activity is up to standard.

 At The CPD Group, we use our

 A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Frameworks

 to make sure that each and every activity we accredit meets a strict set of standards to ensure the highest quality learning experience for the end user.

 Different CPD accreditation bodies will use different criteria to assess their activities. When choosing a CPD activity, or choosing which accreditation body to accredit your own CPD activities with, it is important to establish the accreditation process and whether it is robust and legitimate.

##
 What are the benefits?

###
 Why is CPD Accreditation Important...

#### For Learners?

Stamp of Approval

 It serves as a stamp of approval so learners can see that the activity has been assessed for quality.

Quality Assurance

 It means that learners know they will receive a learning experience that is up to CPD standards.

Accredited Certificates

 Learners will receive an accredited certificate to display their professional development.

CPD Credits

 Learners will obtain

 CPD Credits

 upon completing the activity that they can add to their professional portfolio and log with their employer or regulatory body.

#### For Training Providers?

Customer Confidence

 It gives potential customers confidence that they will receive a great learning experience.

Marketing Tool

 A great marketing tool that demonstrates your activity reaches (or exceeds!) CPD standards.

Course Development

 You will receive feedback on your course if it is not up to standard so you can improve your course.

Build Trust

 By getting accredited, you'll be sending out the message that your activities can be trusted to deliver quality.

## Is CPD Accreditation worth it?

Yes, CPD accreditation is absolutely worth it!

 CPD accreditation is an important tool that helps to raise CPD standards across the board and ensure consistent quality. It makes sure that learners aren't misled when purchasing courses, and enables training providers to demonstrate that their training is trustworthy.
