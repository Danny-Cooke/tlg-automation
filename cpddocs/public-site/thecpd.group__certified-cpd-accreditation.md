 What Does This Certification Mean?

 This certification demonstrates our commitment to delivering high
 standards of CPD accreditation and reflects our dedication to quality learning.

 After successfully meeting The CPD Register's criteria, we are proud to
 be certified against its published standards.

 This milestone reinforces our
 mission to support CPD providers with a transparent and credible process that raises

##
 Explore our Certified Accreditations!

 All of our CPD accreditation services are now certified! Take a look at the wide range of certified accreditation services we offer below:

####
 CPD Provider

 This FREE approval assesses a company or training provider to ensure all the processes are in place to deliver great CPD. Once approved, training providers can go on to accredit activities.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Courses

 We assess courses against our
 A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework
 to ensure quality. Once course accreditation is granted, each course is listed on The CPD Register and will carry a specific amount of trackable CPD Credits.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Trainers

 This accreditation verifies an individual's ability to effectively deliver CPD courses, ensuring they have the relevant expertise and qualifications. Once accredited, Trainers receive an exclusive CPD Trainer Card.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Webinars

 This specialised accreditation is formulated for online seminars, known as webinars. Webinar attendees will be able to scan a QR code at the end of the webinar and receive CPD credits and a certificate.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Podcasts

 We now have a specialised service to accredit Audio based learning! Each podcast will receive a unique link and accreditation code for listeners to use to collect CPD minutes and an accredited certificate. Completely Admin free!

 Learn More

####
 CPD Edutorials

 This accreditation assesses articles within publications and accredits them to ensure a quality learning experience. Readers will be able to scan a bespoke QR code and collect CPD minutes and a CPD certificate.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Employers

 We accredit and endorse employers that go above and beyond to engage their employees in great CPD. This is a FREE recognition for employers that value professional development for their staff.

 Learn More

####
 CPD Events

 This FREE accreditation provides credibility for conferences, exhibitions, lecture series, trade shows, and more. We provide bespoke QR codes for attendees to scan and receive a FREE CPD certificate and CPD credits.

 Learn More
