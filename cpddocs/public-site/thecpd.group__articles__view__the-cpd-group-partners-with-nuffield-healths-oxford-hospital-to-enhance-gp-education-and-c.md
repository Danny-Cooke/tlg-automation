 The CPD Group Partners with Nuffield Health's Oxford Hospital to Enhance GP Education and Career Development

 Jess Hart

 February 17, 2025

 4 min read

 Share:

The CPD Group is excited to announce their partnership with Nuffield Health’s The Manor Hospital, Oxford.

The partnership is aimed at supporting GP education and career development. The collaboration will ensure that GPs have access to high-quality CPD (Continuing Professional Development) events, enabling them to expand their knowledge and refine their skills through direct engagement with medical specialists.

A spokesperson from The Manor Hospital, Anuja Teli, expressed her thoughts about the partnership “We are excited to partner with The CPD Group to support GP education and career development. At our hospital, specialist consultants manage a wide range of cases, but a patient’s first point of contact is often their GP. Through CPD events hosted by the hospital, GPs can learn directly from specialists and apply these insights in their practices, ultimately enhancing the care they provide.”

### Supporting Medical Excellence

Nuffield Health’s Manor Hospital, Oxford, has built an international reputation for excellence, providing top-tier services in neurosurgery, gynaecology, orthopaedics, and cancer care. The hospital also features a specialist paediatric ward, a seven-bedded intensive care unit, and a six-bedded post-anaesthetic care unit (PACU), ensuring comprehensive and compassionate patient care.

### Accreditation and Quality Assurance

As a leader in CPD accreditation, The CPD Group is dedicated to ensuring that learning experiences the highest CPD standards. In this partnership, The CPD Group will not only accredit CPD events hosted by The Manor Hospital but also work closely with them to accredit their CPD trainers. This will provide GPs with confidence in the quality and relevance of the training, helping them stay at the cutting edge of medical advancements.

By partnering with The Manor Hospital, The CPD Group aim to elevate education for GPs

### Elevating GP Education

GPs play a vital role as the first point of contact for patients seeking medical advice and treatment. Attending CPD events accredited by The CPD Group provides GPs with the opportunity to learn directly from leading specialists, stay updated with the latest medical advancements, refine their skills, and deliver even higher-quality care to their patients.

### Raising Standards

This partnership further demonstrates The CPD Group’s commitment to raising the standards for professional growth in the healthcare sector. By working with The Manor Hospital, we aim to ensure that GPs receive the best educational support available, keeping them well-equipped to navigate the evolving medical landscape and deliver exceptional patient care.

Stay tuned for more updates on upcoming CPD events and training opportunities as this collaboration continues to grow.
