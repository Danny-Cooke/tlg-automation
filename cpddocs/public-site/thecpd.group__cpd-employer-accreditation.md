## What it is

### CPD employer accreditation

 CPD employer accreditation is the formal recognition of an organisation's commitment to Continuing Professional Development for its employees. The CPD Group's employer accreditation assesses and approves an organisation's internal training and staff development programmes, confirming that they meet recognised CPD standards and that employees can earn verifiable CPD credits for the learning they complete as part of their employment.

 Employer accreditation from The CPD Group is awarded to businesses that demonstrate a genuine and structured commitment to professional development — not just to training provision. It recognises organisations that invest meaningfully in their people and that can evidence the quality and structure of that investment.

## A different model

### What is CPD employer accreditation?

 Most CPD accreditation is directed at training providers — external organisations that sell courses and learning activities to other businesses and professionals. Employer accreditation is different. It is specifically designed for organisations that want to have their own internal training programmes formally assessed and recognised as meeting CPD standards.

 When an organisation's training is CPD accredited by The CPD Group, employees who complete that training can receive CPD credits and certificates as evidence of their professional development. This is particularly valuable for employees working in regulated professions who need to demonstrate annual CPD to their professional body or regulator — but it applies across all industries where professional development is valued.

## The business case

### The benefits of CPD employer accreditation

#### Employees can evidence their CPD

 For employees in regulated professions, being able to log CPD earned through workplace training is a significant practical benefit. Employer accreditation from The CPD Group means that in-house training generates verifiable CPD credits that employees can include in their annual CPD records. This reduces the time and cost employees spend sourcing external CPD to meet their professional body requirements.

#### Recruitment and retention

 Professional development is consistently ranked among the top factors influencing both job choice and employee retention. Research shows that a significant proportion of professionals would stay with their current employer if meaningful training and development opportunities were available. Displaying The CPD Group's employer accreditation mark signals to current and prospective employees that the organisation takes professional development seriously — and that it has been assessed by The CPD Group.

#### Validation of training quality

 Internal training programmes are often developed with good intentions but without formal quality assurance. The CPD Group's employer accreditation process provides an individual review of the quality, structure, and learning outcomes of your training. This assessment helps HR and L&D teams demonstrate the value of their programmes to senior leadership and to the business as a whole.

#### Enhanced employer brand

 Being a CPD-accredited employer is a visible and meaningful signal of corporate commitment to professional development. It can be featured prominently in recruitment campaigns, employer brand communications, staff handbooks, and LinkedIn company profiles. In sectors where professional development is expected and valued, it positions the organisation as a serious and credible employer.

#### Support for regulatory compliance

 In industries where CPD is mandatory — healthcare, financial services, legal, construction — employer accreditation helps organisations demonstrate regulatory compliance. When employees' professional development requirements can be met through internally accredited training, it reduces the administrative burden of tracking and evidencing CPD across a workforce.

## FAQs

### About employer accreditation

 Can small businesses apply for employer accreditation?

Yes. The CPD Group works with organisations of all sizes. Whether you have a dedicated L&D team or a single person responsible for training, employer accreditation is accessible provided your training meets the required quality standards.

 Can specific training programmes be accredited rather than the entire training offer?

Yes. Employer accreditation can be applied to specific programmes, modules, or training initiatives rather than the organisation's entire training portfolio. The CPD Group's team can advise on the most appropriate scope for your organisation.

 How long does employer accreditation last?

Employer accreditation is renewed annually, ensuring that recognised training programmes continue to meet current CPD standards. The CPD Group sends renewal reminders in advance and the renewal process is designed to be straightforward.
