## Who we are

### Become a CPD Provider with The CPD Group

 Becoming a CPD Provider is the first step towards having your training, courses, events, and other learning activities individually assessed and accredited by an established UK CPD accreditation body. The CPD Group is the first and currently only accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, and has been awarded the Most Trusted CPD Accreditation Service by the UK Business Excellence Awards. With over 13,000 approved providers worldwide, joining The CPD Group means joining a recognised, credible, and growing community of professional learning organisations.

 CPD stands for Continuing Professional Development. It is the ongoing process of learning and professional growth that professionals across all industries undertake throughout their careers. When your organisation becomes an approved CPD Provider with The CPD Group, you demonstrate to your clients, delegates, and professional audiences that your training has been assessed against recognised quality standards.

## The process

### What does becoming a CPD Provider involve?

 Becoming a CPD Provider with The CPD Group is completely free. The provider approval process involves an initial assessment of your organisation to confirm that your business is ready to deliver CPD-accredited learning. This means verifying that you have the professional structure, subject matter expertise, and commitment to quality that CPD standards require.

 Once approved, your organisation receives a unique CPD Provider logo, a verifiable provider number, a CPD Provider certificate, and automatic listing on The CPD Register, where learners and employers can verify your accreditation status. Provider approval opens the door to submitting individual activities for accreditation assessment.

## Why apply

### The benefits of becoming an approved provider

#### Credibility

 When your organisation carries The CPD Group's provider approval, it signals to professional audiences that your training has been assessed by a certified accreditation body. The CPD Group is the only UK accreditation body certified by The CPD Register, which means your provider status is supported by that certification.

#### Discoverability on The CPD Register

 Approved providers are automatically listed on The CPD Register, a public directory of accredited CPD providers. Professionals and employers actively search The CPD Register when looking for quality-assured training, giving your organisation increased visibility among the audiences that matter most.

#### Access to accreditation for individual activities

 Provider approval is the gateway to having your courses, webinars, events, and other activities individually accredited. Each accredited activity receives its own unique logo and verifiable accreditation number, allowing delegates to receive CPD certificates and log their learning.

#### Marketing advantage

 Displaying The CPD Group's provider logo on your website, course pages, and marketing materials signals quality and professionalism to potential clients. In sectors where CPD is mandatory — including healthcare, law, finance, and engineering — the ability to state that your training is CPD-accredited is a significant commercial differentiator.

#### Part of a trusted community

 With over 13,000 approved providers, The CPD Group's provider community spans every major industry and sector. Becoming an approved provider connects your organisation to that community and to the network of professionals, employers, and institutions that recognise The CPD Group's accreditation mark.

## FAQs

### About becoming a CPD Provider

 Is it free to become a CPD Provider?

Yes. Provider approval with The CPD Group is completely free. Fees apply when individual activities — such as courses, webinars, or events — are submitted for accreditation assessment. The CPD Group offers flexible pricing packages to suit organisations of all sizes.

 How long does provider approval take?

The CPD Group aims to process provider applications promptly. Once you have submitted your application, the team will review your organisation and be in touch to discuss your requirements and confirm your approval.

 Do I need to be a large organisation to apply?

No. The CPD Group works with individual trainers and coaches, small businesses, and large multinational corporations alike. What matters is the quality of your learning, not the size of your organisation.

 What's the difference between being a CPD Provider and having a CPD-accredited activity?

Provider approval recognises your organisation as being ready to deliver CPD-accredited learning. Activity accreditation is the separate process of having an individual course, webinar, event, or other activity assessed and approved against CPD standards. You need to be an approved provider before your activities can be individually accredited.
