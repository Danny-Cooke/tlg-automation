#
 The CPD Register: What Is It?

 The CPD Register is an independent, easily searchable directory of all things CPD. The CPD Register
 makes it easy for learners to verify a CPD Activity, find a CPD Provider that offers the learning
 they need, authenticate a CPD Trainer, or just see what's out there.

 If you want your services to be discoverable, you need to be listed. This is why we automatically
 list all of our accredited Providers, Trainers, and Activities on The CPD Register.

## CPD Register Benefits

 We automatically list all CPD Accredited Providers, Trainers, and Activities on The CPD Register. But
 what are the benefits?

### Showcase Services

 Showcase your CPD Accredited services to thousands of customers looking for high quality CPD around
 the world.

### Verify Activities

 Allow your learners to verify that your activities have been fully assessed and accredited by a
 trusted CPD Accreditation Body.

### Authenticate Yourself

 Link your students straight to the register to authenticate your trainer status! We're championing
 you, so show it off!

### Quality Assured

 Every service on the register has been fully assessed, so your learners can be assured that your
 learning adheres to high standards.

### Industry Standards

 Make sure you're meeting industry standards by showcasing your services on the #1 destination for
 all things CPD.

### Growing Community

 The CPD Register is a rapidly growing online community of CPD Providers - join them today by getting
 accredited with us.

 Who is Listed on The CPD Register?

 We're the only CPD Accreditation Body to automatically list our Providers, Trainers, and Activities on The CPD Register. Join them by getting accredited with us today!

#### CPD Providers

 If your company has been accredited as a CPD Provider with us, you'll appear on the CPD Register.
