 We've Approved 10,000 CPD Providers!

#
 We've Approved 10,000 CPD Providers!

 Jess Hart

 March 26, 2025

 3 min read

 Share:

You made it happen.

We've hit a major milestone.

The CPD Group have approved over 10,000 CPD Providers from all around the world!

We would be lying if we said we weren't a little bit proud! 🥹

This is a huge milestone in our mission to raise the standards of CPD around the world and support high quality professional development from all industries.

We couldn't have done it without you; our ever growing community of CPD professionals.

This isn't all about numbers, though. This is about the 10,000 businesses that are making a difference to the world of professional development. 10,000 business with commitment to quality learning, credible training, and sharing new skills.

### This is only the beginning

We're only just getting started on our journey to make CPD better for everyone.

We have another exciting announcement coming soon that we can't wait to share with you; so be sure to keep an eye out over the next few weeks. 👀

### Meet our 10,000th Approved Provider! 👋

Say hello to our 10,000th approved provider!

🥁 Drum roll please...

It's PARK VIEW EVENTS LTD 🎉

Park View Events are the organisers of The Autism Show - a series of incredible exhibitions held in London, Birmingham, and Manchester. These events offer essential training, resources, and support for autistic and ADHD individuals and those who support them.

Learn more about The Autism Show here!

### Be part of something important

If you're a training business who wants to boost your credibility and even more value to your training, we've got you covered.

Join 10,000 training businesses who are already making a difference by signing up for FREE.

Once you're all signed up, our team will get in touch for a chat about your business, and tell you more about the accreditation journey.

### Let's celebrate!

If you're an approved provider that's part of our 10k strong community, we want to celebrate you!

Share the achievement! Let your network know you’re part of the milestone by tagging us on socials and using the #CPDGROUP10k hashtag. 🎉

Together - we're shaping the future of CPD; setting new standards, and making professional development more accessible and effective than ever before.

From the whole of The CPD Group team, THANK YOU! 💛

Looking for CPD Accreditation?

We accredit a wide range of CPD activities, so take a look at the different types of accreditation we offer to find what suits you best:

- CPD Courses

- CPD Webinars

- CPD Edutorials

- CPD Audio Podcasts

- CPD Video Podcasts

- CPD Accredited Events

- CPD Trainers

- CPD Employers

Every activity we accredit is assessed individually against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework and listed on The CPD Register, where you can verify its accreditation status before you commit.

How to become an accredited CPD Provider

We hope this article was helpful. The CPD Group is the first CPD accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, working with over 13,000 approved providers across 170+ countries and 100+ industries. Every course, webinar, event or podcast we accredit is assessed on its own merit, never as part of a blanket approval.

If you're delivering training, running events, hosting a podcast, or creating any learning content that offers genuine professional development value, we'd love to help you get it accredited. Visit our Become a CPD Provider page or contact our team for a friendly chat about what we can do for you. If you're a learner looking to track, store and verify your own CPD, create your free CPD Passport - it connects directly to The CPD Register for instant verification of your accredited learning.
