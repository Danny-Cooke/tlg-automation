The CPD Group: Member Update

Through our Member Report mechanism and subsequent investigation, we have withdrawn the accreditation for the following organisation:

Organisation Name: Skin Clinic London

Approved CPD Provider Number: 781820

Reason for CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal: Breach of The CPD Group's Terms and Conditions, under section 6. Termination

CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal Date: 16th January 2025

Background:

The above organisation gained Approved CPD Provider status from The CPD Group on the 27th of June 2023.

Approved Provider status demonstrates that a company has been reviewed by The CPD Group, and we have deemed the organisation ‘CPD Ready’ as they have all the fundamental elements in place to deliver high-quality CPD. This in no way demonstrates that the organisation has gained any form of CPD Accreditation form The CPD Group for their CPD provision

Listed below are the CPD Training courses that Skin Clinic London had CPD Accredited by us:

·     1015418 - Micro needling – Accreditation Date: 14th March 2024

·     1015438 - Hydro facial – Accreditation Date: 18th March 2024

·     1015467 - High frequency – Accreditation Date: 19th March 2024

·     1015468 – Dermaplaning – Accreditation Date: 19th March 2024

**CPD Accreditation for all the above listed course titles has been revoked as of 16th January 2025 – Any courses delivered after the 16th January 2025 are NOT CPD Accredited by The CPD Group**

The above listed subjects are the ONLY CPD Training titles and subjects that have been CPD Accredited by The CPD Group. If any member of the public is in receipt of certification from Skin Clinic London that presents one of the above listed titles or subjects, between the above outlined ‘Accreditation Date’ and prior to 16th January 2025, your certification holds valid CPD Accreditation and is not affected by this withdrawal of services.

However, if any member of the public is in receipt of certification from Skin Clinic London that presents an alternative course title or subject, or it is dated prior to the above listed ‘Accreditation Date’ or post 16th January 2025, you can be assured it does not hold CPD Accreditation from The CPD Group.

However, if any member of the public is in receipt of certification from Skin Clinic London that presents an alternative course title or subject, or it is dated post 16th January 2025, you can be assured it does not hold CPD Accreditation from The CPD Group.

However, please be advised that this does not mean that the course was not Accredited as Skin Clinic London may have the specific course title or subject CPD Accredited by an alternative CPD Accreditation Body.

We would highly recommend contacting the Training Provider directly with your enquires.

Please Note: If any member of the public has experienced issues with the non-delivery of goods or machinery you must contact the training provider directly as the CPD Accreditation applied to each CPD Course by The CPD Group is in no way related to any products, goods, or machinery, or any such item, offered as part of a 'package'.

The CPD Group take all comments, feedback, complaints, concerns, queries good, bad or indifferent extremely seriously in order to ensure high standards of CPD and will fully investigate all reports thoroughly.
