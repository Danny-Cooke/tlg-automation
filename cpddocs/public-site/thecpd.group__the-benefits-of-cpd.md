#
 The Benefits Of CPD

 What are the benefits of CPD Provider Approval?

##
 Welcome to the CPD Group!

###
 Join our Community!

 At The CPD Group, we want to go that extra mile to help our customers thrive. For us, accreditation is so much more than just a logo. With The CPD Group, you're joining a community of experts that are passionate about great learning and want to raise the bar for professional development.

 We're always on hand if you need some support or just want some more information about CPD approval. Get in touch with our friendly team via phone, email, live chat, or message us on our socials.

##
 CPD Provider Benefits

###
 What do you get?

 Getting approved as a CPD Provider assesses your company as a whole, ensuring that you are CPD Ready. This process comes with many benefits. For example, you'll receive a

 CPD Provider

 certificate which you can use to display your approval, as well as an accredited logo that you can use across your social media, website, promotional materials and more!

 You'll also be listed on The

 CPD Register

 - the searchable directory of all things CPD. The CPD Register is where people go to find great CPD. By getting listed, you'll be seen by thousands of potential customers searching for learning and development!

#### What you'll receive:

 CPD Provider Certificate

 CPD Approved Logo

 Listed on The CPD Register

Did you know?

 We're the only accreditation body to offer this service for free!

##
 More than just accreditation

###
 Access our Resources Hub!

 If you log into your account, you'll find our resources hub. Here, you'll find all sorts of useful documents that have been created to help your business thrive. At The CPD Group, we strongly advise all of our

 CPD Providers

 to have a range of policies in place to ensure exemplary business practice.

 To help with this, we provide a range of free templates that you can apply to your business.

 For example, as a business that provides learning, you will benefit from having a policy on reviewing your course content regularly to ensure quality, as well as having measures in place to safeguard learners.

 We also have template policies for things that every business should have, such as a Data Protection Policy, Complaints policies, Equal Opportunities Policy and lots more!

 You'll also find a range of helpful guides and resources that cover intellectual property - which is an important component of every company. For example, we have guides on Copyright, Intellectual Property, Patents, Trademarks and more.
