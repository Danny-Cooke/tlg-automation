 The CPD Group Become the First Certified CPD Accreditation Body

 Jess Hart

 March 12, 2025

 4 min read

 Share:

### We’re Certified! 🎉

We are thrilled to announce that The CPD Group is now the first (and currently the only) certified CPD accreditation organisation in the world! This landmark achievement reinforces our position as a trusted leader in CPD accreditation, dedicated to upholding and advancing industry standards.

The CPD Group are the first accreditation body to receive certification

### What Does This Certification Mean? 🤔

The CPD Group have achieved certification through The CPD Register.

The CPD Register has established itself as a certification body that assesses CPD accreditation providers like us to ensure they meet the highest standards of quality, methodology, and compliance.

After successfully meeting The CPD Register's criteria, we are proud to offer accreditation services that meet internationally recognised industry standards.

This certification mark serves as an assurance that our accreditation processes adhere to strict industry criteria, enhancing trust and credibility across the world of CPD.

This is another yet another milestone in our mission to raise CPD standards across the globe. CPD providers and learners alike can have increased confidence that our standards are internationally recognised and verified.

### What Does This Mean for Our Customers?

This not only great news for us, but, more importantly, it’s great news for our community of CPD providers. You are now able to say that you're approved or accredited by the only certified accreditation body out there.

Pretty cool right? Be sure to shout about it, it's something to be proud of!

If you’re accredited by The CPD Group, you can now proudly state that your accreditation comes from the only certified CPD accreditation organisation in the UK. This adds even more value to your accreditation, ensuring that your CPD activities are recognised as meeting the highest industry benchmarks.

Learn more about the criteria we have met here: CPD Accreditation Certification Criteria.

### Choosing the Right CPD Accreditation Provider

For those who hold CPD accreditation from another provider, this certification raises important questions...

Is your accreditation body certified? Are they in the process of becoming certified? If not, why not?

Choosing an accreditation body that has been through certification ensures that your CPD activities meet the highest recognised standards. We encourage CPD providers to ask questions to their current accreditation provider and consider the benefits of working with a certified organisation like The CPD Group.

### Thank you!

We'd like to round off this article by saying a HUGE thank our valued customers for their continued trust and support; we couldn't do what we do without you.

Together, we are driving higher standards in professional development worldwide, and this certification is as much yours as it is ours! 💛

We welcome any questions about CPD accreditation, our certification process, and what this means for you. Reach out to us via phone, email, or live chat, we’re always here to help.

Looking for CPD Accreditation?

- CPD Courses

Every activity we accredit is assessed individually against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework and listed on The CPD Register, where you can verify its accreditation status before you commit.

How to become an accredited CPD Provider

We hope this article was helpful. The CPD Group is the first CPD accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, working with over 13,000 approved providers across 170+ countries and 100+ industries. Every course, webinar, event or podcast we accredit is assessed on its own merit, never as part of a blanket approval.

If you're delivering training, running events, hosting a podcast, or creating any learning content that offers genuine professional development value, we'd love to help you get it accredited. Visit our Become a CPD Provider page or contact our team for a friendly chat about what we can do for you. If you're a learner looking to track, store and verify your own CPD, create your free CPD Passport - it connects directly to The CPD Register for instant verification of your accredited learning.
