## What it is

### CPD webinar accreditation

 CPD webinar accreditation is the process of having a webinar individually assessed and approved as a Continuing Professional Development activity. When a webinar is accredited by The CPD Group, it confirms that the content meets recognised professional development standards and that delegates can earn CPD credits and receive a verifiable CPD certificate for attending.

 The CPD Group accredits webinars for training providers, coaches, event organisers, subject matter experts, and businesses across all industries. We assess both live webinars and pre-recorded webinar content. Our accreditation process is designed to be practical and straightforward, with each submission reviewed individually by our specialist team.

## The mechanics

### What is CPD webinar accreditation?

 A webinar is a video-based learning session delivered online, either live or on demand. CPD webinar accreditation confirms that the session delivers genuine professional development value — that it has clear learning objectives, relevant content, a qualified presenter, and a means by which attendees can evidence their participation.

 When your webinar is accredited by The CPD Group, your delegates can use a QR code to download a personalised CPD certificate at the end of the session. This certificate confirms the number of CPD credits earned and includes your accreditation number, which can be verified on The CPD Register.

## The 4-step process

### How does CPD webinar accreditation work?

The CPD Group's webinar accreditation process follows four stages.

1

#### Become a CPD Provider

Before accrediting your webinar, your organisation needs to be registered as an approved CPD Provider. This is free and confirms your organisation is ready to deliver CPD-accredited learning.

2

#### Submit your webinar details

You provide a detailed summary of your webinar content, the learning objectives, the duration, and the credentials of the presenter.

3

#### Assessment

The CPD Group's team assesses the expertise of the delivery team, the quality of the content, and the learning outcomes to confirm the webinar meets CPD standards.

4

#### Accreditation and QR code

Once accredited, you receive a unique QR code to display at your webinar. Delegates scan the code to receive their personalised CPD certificate, making the process seamless and immediate.

## Why it matters

### The benefits of CPD webinar accreditation

#### Delegates earn verifiable CPD credits

 Many professionals need to demonstrate their CPD to an employer or professional body each year. When your webinar is CPD accredited, delegates can include it in their professional development record and provide verifiable evidence of their attendance and learning. This makes your webinar significantly more attractive to professional audiences.

#### Increased credibility and discoverability

 Accredited webinars are listed on The CPD Register, a public directory of CPD activities. Professionals and employers actively search The CPD Register when looking for quality-assured learning, increasing your audience reach. Displaying The CPD Group's accreditation mark in your webinar promotion and on your website signals quality assurance.

#### Seamless certificate delivery via QR code

 The CPD Group's QR code system means delegates can access their CPD certificate immediately during or after the webinar, without any administrative effort from your team. Certificates are personalised, branded, and contain a verifiable accreditation number.

#### Competitive advantage

 In a crowded market for online professional development content, CPD accreditation differentiates your webinars from unaccredited alternatives. For providers targeting professionals in regulated industries — healthcare, law, finance, engineering — accreditation is often a prerequisite for attendance.

## FAQs

### About webinar accreditation

 Can pre-recorded webinars be CPD accredited?

Yes. Both live and pre-recorded webinars can be submitted for accreditation. The assessment criteria are the same regardless of delivery format — what matters is the quality of the learning content and objectives.

 Do delegates need to do anything to receive their CPD certificate?

Delegates simply scan the QR code provided by The CPD Group at the end of the webinar. They enter their details and their personalised certificate is generated immediately. No manual certificate processing is required from the provider.

 How many CPD credits does a webinar earn?

CPD credits are calculated based on the duration of the webinar. As a general rule, one CPD credit is equivalent to one hour of structured learning. A 60-minute webinar would typically earn one CPD credit. Your accreditation certificate will specify the exact number of credits.
