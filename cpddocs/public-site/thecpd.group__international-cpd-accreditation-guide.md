## A global standard

### International CPD accreditation

 The CPD Group provides internationally recognised CPD accreditation for training providers, event organisers, coaches, and employers operating across all global markets. We work with over 13,000 approved providers from countries including the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, the UAE, South Africa, India, Singapore, and throughout Europe. Our accreditation is accepted by professionals and organisations worldwide as a recognised mark of CPD quality.

 CPD — Continuing Professional Development — is a globally understood concept, though it is known by different names in different regions. In the USA it is commonly called Continuing Education (CE) or Continuing Professional Education (CPE). In medical contexts it may be Continuing Medical Education (CME). In legal contexts, Continuing Legal Education (CLE). Regardless of terminology, the underlying principle — structured, verifiable, ongoing professional learning — is consistent, and CPD accreditation from The CPD Group is recognised as meeting that standard internationally.

## Region by region

### CPD around the world

How CPD operates across the major regions where The CPD Group's accreditation is recognised.

#### United Kingdom

 CPD

 The UK has one of the most mature and structured CPD frameworks in the world. Over 450 professional bodies require their members to complete CPD annually. The CPD Group is a UK-based, UK-registered organisation and our accreditation is fully recognised across all UK professional sectors and regulatory bodies.

#### United States of America

 CE
 CPE

 CPD in the USA is most commonly called Continuing Education (CE) or Continuing Professional Education (CPE). There are over 4,000 professional bodies and regulatory organisations across the USA with CE requirements for their members. CPD accreditation from The CPD Group is recognised as a quality mark by US-based professionals and organisations, particularly those working for multinational companies or in sectors with global professional standards.

#### Australia and New Zealand

 CPD

 Australia and New Zealand follow a CPD model closely aligned with the UK. Many professions have mandatory CPD requirements set by their regulatory bodies and professional institutes. CPD accreditation from The CPD Group is accepted by Australian and New Zealand professionals, many of whom work for organisations with UK or global professional standards.

#### United Arab Emirates & the Middle East

 CPD

 The UAE and wider Middle East region have a strong and growing emphasis on professional development, with significant demand for internationally recognised CPD. Many professionals in the region hold UK professional qualifications and memberships that require UK-recognised CPD. The CPD Group works with a number of UAE and Middle East-based training providers and has accredited events and conferences held across the region.

#### Europe

 Varies by country

 CPD requirements across Europe vary significantly by country and profession. However, the increasing internationalisation of professional standards — particularly in healthcare, law, financial services, and engineering — means that internationally recognised CPD accreditation from The CPD Group is valued across European markets.

#### Africa

 Emerging
 Growing

 CPD is an emerging and growing practice across the African continent, with particular emphasis in South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, and Ghana. The CPD Group works with providers based in or delivering into Africa, and our accreditation is recognised by professionals across the continent who hold UK or international professional memberships.
