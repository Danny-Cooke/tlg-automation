#
 The CPD Group Wins Most Trusted Accreditation Service Award.

 Jess Hart

 June 17, 2024

 3 min read

 Share:

We've got incredible news to share. The CPD Group has been named The Most Trusted CPD Accreditation Service in the UK Acquisition International Magazine 's Business Excellence Awards!

This prestigious award recognises the outstanding achievements of businesses across the UK. Earning this recognition fills us with immense pride, as it reflects the trust the education and training community has placed in our services.

The CPD Group team are thrilled to have won this award.

### Building Trust Through Quality 🤝

Trust is the foundation of the entire CPD accreditation process. Learners rely on accreditation to ensure their chosen activities meet the highest quality standards and deliver valuable learning outcomes. Our rigorous evaluation procedures ensure professionals can confidently invest their time and resources in continuing education.

Training providers also entrust us with their expertise. When they submit their courses for accreditation, they know we'll provide a fair and objective assessment, ultimately adding credibility and value to their offerings.

### A Commitment to Excellence 🏆

Here at The CPD Group, we're passionate about upholding the highest CPD accreditation standards. We've streamlined our processes for maximum efficiency, all while maintaining a robust and respected evaluation system across all our services. Our ultimate goal is to elevate CPD standards on a global scale, empowering those high-quality educational experiences that empower professional and personal development.

### Thank You for Being Part of Our Journey!

This award wouldn't be possible without the incredible support of our amazing customers!

A massive THANK YOU to each and every one of you for trusting The CPD Group to deliver the very best in CPD accreditation services. Since 2017, we've welcomed over 7,000 businesses from around the world into our network – a testament to our shared passion for exceptional learning.

Together, we're raising the bar for CPD excellence, globally! 🌎

Looking for CPD Accreditation?

We accredit a wide range of CPD activities, so take a look at the different types of accreditation we offer to find what suits you best:

- CPD Courses

- CPD Webinars

- CPD Edutorials

- CPD Audio Podcasts

- CPD Video Podcasts

- CPD Accredited Events

- CPD Trainers

- CPD Employers

Every activity we accredit is assessed individually against our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework and listed on The CPD Register, where you can verify its accreditation status before you commit.

How to become an accredited CPD Provider

We hope this article was helpful. The CPD Group is the first CPD accreditation organisation to be certified by The CPD Register, working with over 13,000 approved providers across 170+ countries and 100+ industries. Every course, webinar, event or podcast we accredit is assessed on its own merit, never as part of a blanket approval.

If you're delivering training, running events, hosting a podcast, or creating any learning content that offers genuine professional development value, we'd love to help you get it accredited. Visit our Become a CPD Provider page or contact our team for a friendly chat about what we can do for you. If you're a learner looking to track, store and verify your own CPD, create your free CPD Passport - it connects directly to The CPD Register for instant verification of your accredited learning.
