# How We Help You Get CPD Accredited: Your Complete Three-Stage Journey

Achieving CPD accreditation for your training organisation doesn't need to be complicated. At The CPD Group, we've developed a straightforward three-stage process that supports CPD providers from initial account creation through to full activity accreditation. As the first certified CPD accreditation organisation, we're here to guide you through every step of becoming an accredited CPD provider whilst ensuring your courses meet our rigorous quality standards.

## Understanding Our Three-Stage CPD Accreditation Process

In our work with training providers across sectors, we've found that quality CPD accreditation requires a structured yet supportive approach. That's why we've created a three-stage system that ensures your organisation is genuinely ready to deliver accredited CPD learning whilst making the process as smooth and accessible as possible for training providers committed to quality professional development.

Our three-stage accreditation journey begins with free account creation and progresses through provider approval to individual course accreditation. Each stage serves a specific purpose in ensuring that only organisations genuinely capable of delivering valuable professional development receive CPD accreditation, and that the CPD courses they offer meet our comprehensive quality framework for professional learning.

## Stage 1: Create Your Free Account

Your journey to CPD accreditation begins with creating a free account on our platform. This straightforward process takes just minutes and opens the door to our comprehensive CPD accreditation services. Visit our Training Provider page at https://thecpd.group/training-provider and complete the simple registration form with your basic contact details and organisational information.

Creating your account establishes your presence in The CPD Group's network of training providers and gives you access to our provider portal. This portal becomes your central hub for managing CPD accreditation applications, tracking the status of your submissions, and accessing resources that support your professional development offerings. There are no fees for account creation - we believe quality CPD accreditation should be accessible to training organisations of all sizes.

Once your account is created, you'll receive access to guidance materials that explain our CPD accreditation framework, outline the provider approval process, and help you prepare for the next stages of becoming an accredited CPD provider. This preparatory stage ensures you understand what's required to demonstrate that your organisation is CPD ready and capable of delivering quality professional development.

## Stage 2: Complete Your Free Provider Approval Application

The second stage involves completing your free provider approval application. This comprehensive assessment evaluates your organisation's capacity to deliver quality professional development and ensures you meet the fundamental requirements for becoming an approved CPD provider. Through this application, we examine your training expertise, your understanding of CPD principles, your organisational policies, and your commitment to maintaining quality standards in professional learning.

The provider approval application asks about your organisation's background, your experience delivering training and professional development, your quality assurance processes, and your approach to learner support. You'll need to provide details about your company structure, upload relevant policies (such as complaints procedures and data protection policies), and demonstrate that your organisation possesses the necessary infrastructure to deliver CPD courses effectively.

We review provider applications promptly, typically responding within five working days. Our assessment team examines your submission thoroughly to understand your capabilities and determine whether you meet the criteria for becoming an Approved CPD Provider. This isn't a gatekeeping exercise - we're looking to support training organisations that demonstrate genuine commitment to quality professional development. If we have questions or need additional information about your CPD training capabilities, we'll reach out directly to discuss your application and provide guidance on any areas that need strengthening.

Once approved, you receive your Approved CPD Provider status completely free. This status includes a unique provider approval logo, certificate, and approval number that you can use to display your organisation's CPD Provider credentials. Your organisation will be automatically listed on The CPD Register, making you discoverable to professionals searching for quality CPD training. This Approved Provider status remains free throughout your relationship with us - there are no annual fees, renewal charges, or hidden costs for maintaining your provider accreditation.

## Stage 3: Submit Your CPD Courses for Individual Accreditation

With your Approved CPD Provider status secured, you can begin submitting individual CPD courses for accreditation - the third and final stage of our process. Each course submission undergoes thorough evaluation against our comprehensive ACCREDITED framework to ensure it meets recognised CPD standards and delivers genuine value to learners seeking professional development. This stage focuses on the specific quality of your course content, delivery methods, and learning outcomes.

Our ACCREDITED framework examines ten critical dimensions of course quality. We assess Accessibility (can diverse learners access your course?), Content (is your material accurate, current, and relevant?), Creative delivery (does your course engage learners effectively?), Relevance (does the training address genuine professional development needs?), Eligibility requirements (are prerequisites clearly communicated?), Details (is course information comprehensive and clear?), Intellectual Property (do you own or have rights to use your materials?), Testing methods (how do you assess learner progress?), Expertise (does your course author possess appropriate qualifications?), and Duration (is the learning time appropriately allocated?).

This detailed CPD accreditation process ensures that only courses meeting rigorous CPD standards receive accreditation. If your submission requires adjustments, our team provides clear, constructive feedback that helps you enhance your course materials and strengthen your CPD offering. We work collaboratively with CPD providers throughout this process, viewing our role as supporting your success rather than creating barriers to accreditation. Our feedback focuses on helping you understand how specific changes enhance learning outcomes and ensure your CPD course delivers maximum value to participants.

Once your course receives CPD accreditation, it's automatically listed on The CPD Register where professionals can discover and enrol in your training. You receive accreditation materials including course-specific logos, certificates for learners, and promotional resources that help you market your accredited CPD course effectively. The combination of free provider approval and flexible activity accreditation means you can grow your accredited CPD portfolio at a pace that suits your business whilst maintaining the quality standards that distinguish your training.

## Our Supportive Approach to CPD Accreditation

What distinguishes The CPD Group from other CPD accreditation bodies is our partnership mentality throughout all three stages of the process. We don't see ourselves as gatekeepers but as collaborators in quality professional development. When reviewing your organisation during provider approval or evaluating your CPD courses during activity accreditation, we look for strengths to celebrate alongside areas for improvement. Our assessment process focuses on supporting training providers in delivering exceptional professional development rather than simply identifying deficiencies.

If your provider application or course submission requires modifications, we explain precisely what needs to be addressed and why. Our feedback connects improvement suggestions directly to learner outcomes, helping you understand how changes enhance the professional development experience your CPD courses deliver. This supportive approach has enabled us to establish a network of over 11,000 approved CPD providers across 170 countries, all dedicated to delivering high-quality professional learning.

## Why Choose The CPD Group for Your Accreditation Journey

As the first certified CPD accreditation organisation, we've set new standards for quality and credibility in professional development accreditation. Our certification from The CPD Register demonstrates that our accreditation services meet the highest industry standards, giving you confidence that your CPD accreditation is recognised, respected, and properly conducted. Training providers choosing The CPD Group benefit from this certification, enhancing their credibility when marketing accredited CPD courses to professionals.

Our three-stage process balances rigour with accessibility. We maintain high-quality standards through our comprehensive ACCREDITED framework whilst ensuring the accreditation journey remains straightforward and supportive for training organisations committed to professional development excellence. The combination of free provider approval and transparent activity accreditation processes means you can plan your investment in CPD accreditation with confidence, knowing exactly what's required at each stage.
