## What is Audio Podcast Accreditation?

 We provide a thorough accreditation process to ensure the quality of your
 Audio Podcasts for learning purposes. By becoming accredited, you'll demonstrate that your
 Audio Podcast has met the highest CPD standards and undergone a quality assessment.

 Getting your podcasts accredited with us is simple, here's how it works in 4 steps:

 Get Accredited

### Provider

 Before you accredit your Audio Podcast with us, you'll need to become a
 CPD
 Provider
 so we know you're CPD ready.

### Delivery

 We'll assess the expertise of the person delivering the audio podcast,
 ensuring they are qualified to do so.

### Content

 We'll need to see a detailed summary of the audio podcast contents to ensure
 a quality listening experience.

### Unique Code

 We provide a unique accreditation code that allows your listeners to download their CPD certificate.

##
 Provide CPD Minutes

 to Listeners

 With the growing need for professionals across various sectors to fulfill, document, and
 validate their ongoing professional development activities, providing CPD minutes for your
 Audio Podcasts is valuable selling point. After listening to your Audio Podcasts, learners will
 be able
 to record their CPD minutes as evidence of their continuous professional growth. This is
 advantageous to your listeners as it allows them to showcase their new skills and
 achievements in their CVs and share them with employers, regulators, or professional
 organisations.
