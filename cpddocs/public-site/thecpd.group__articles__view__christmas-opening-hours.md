#
 Christmas Opening Hours

 Jess Hart

 December 16, 2024

 1 min read

 Share:

Thank you to everyone for a fantastic year at The CPD Group. Here are our opening hours over Christmas 2024. Please be aware that any materials submitted for accreditation during this period may take longer to process.

🎄 Merry Christmas to you all and here's to a fantastic 2025!

### Christmas Opening Hours 2024:

Monday 16th - Friday 20th December: 08:30 - 17:30

Saturday 21st - Monday 23rd December: CLOSED

Christmas Eve: CLOSED

Christmas Day: CLOSED

Boxing Day until New Year's Day: CLOSED

Thursday 2nd Jan: REOPEN 08:30 - 17:30

If you do have any questions or queries leading up to the festive period, be sure to get in contact before we close via our online chat, telephone, or email.
