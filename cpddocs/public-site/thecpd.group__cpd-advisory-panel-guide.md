## Independent oversight

### The CPD Group Advisory Panel

 The CPD Group is guided and supported by an independent Advisory Panel of professional learning and development experts. The Advisory Panel provides strategic guidance on CPD standards, industry developments, and best practice in professional development — ensuring that The CPD Group's accreditation standards remain rigorous, current, and relevant to the professional sectors we serve.

 The independence and expertise of our Advisory Panel is one of the factors that distinguishes The CPD Group from less rigorous accreditation organisations. Our standards are not set by a single organisation in isolation — they are informed by expert practitioners from across the professional learning landscape, reviewed against current research and industry practice, and independently certified by The CPD Register.

## What they do

### The role of the Advisory Panel

 The CPD Group's Advisory Panel plays a key role in maintaining the integrity and relevance of our accreditation standards. The panel works alongside our internal accreditation team to ensure our standards reflect the realities of professional learning today — not just the policies of a single organisation.

Panel responsibilities

#### Five core activities

Review & advise on assessment criteria and frameworks

Guide on emerging trends in professional learning

Advise on sector-specific CPD requirements & standards

Contribute to development of new accreditation formats & services

Help ensure our approach reflects current best practice

## Why it matters

### Why independent advisory expertise matters

#### Without it, accreditation becomes self-referential

 CPD accreditation without independent expert input risks becoming self-referential — an organisation setting its own standards without external challenge or validation. The CPD Group's Advisory Panel provides the external perspective that keeps our standards genuinely aligned with the needs of professionals, employers, and the sectors we serve.

#### Independent standards, externally validated

 This independent advisory structure is part of what makes The CPD Group's accreditation credible to professional bodies, employers, and regulators. Combined with our certification by The CPD Register — the UK's independent certification body for CPD accreditation organisations — it means our accreditation is grounded in independently validated standards, not just internal policy.

### Standards shaped by sector practitioners, not just policy.

## Our commitment

### The CPD Group's commitment to standards

 The CPD Group was founded with a mission to raise CPD standards across the UK and internationally. That mission requires a genuine commitment to rigour — not just the appearance of quality, but the substance of it. Our Advisory Panel, our A.C.C.R.E.D.I.T.E.D Framework, our individual activity assessment model, and our CPD Register certification all reflect that commitment.

 We are the first and only UK CPD accreditation body to be certified by The CPD Register. We assess every activity individually rather than offering blanket accreditation. And we maintain an independent Advisory Panel to ensure our standards evolve with the sectors we serve. This is what it means to be the most trusted CPD accreditation service in the UK.
