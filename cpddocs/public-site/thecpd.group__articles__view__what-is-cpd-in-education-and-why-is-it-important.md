 What is CPD in Education and Why Is It Important?

#
 What is CPD in Education and Why Is It Important?

 Jess Hart

 May 2, 2023

 4 min read

 Share:

﻿CPD is an important component of education that helps teachers stay up to date with the most recent knowledge in their specialism. It's super important as it allows students to gain the very best standard of education, as educators will be fully equipped with the latest skills and knowledge to help them excel personally and academically.

Continue reading to learn more...

### What does CPD stand for?

CPD stands for Continuing Professional Development and refers to the ongoing process of professionals engaging in activities to enhance their understanding, knowledge, skills and expertise.

CPD is important in every area of Education

﻿

### What does CPD look like in Education?

1) Participating in Conferences, Workshops, and Seminars

Attending CPD gatherings such as these is a fantastic opportunity for educators to explore their professional development outside of the classroom.

These events allow educators to learn about new teaching methods, technologies, and research findings in their field. They can also network with other educators to gain new insights into their teaching practise.

Teachers attending a conference

﻿

2) Obtaining Additional Qualifications or Certifications

Educators may choose to obtain additional qualifications or certifications in order to improve their knowledge and skills in a specific subject area or teaching method. For example, there has recently been a rise in the use of augmented and virtual reality in education. Teachers may find it beneficial to take a course on this so they can utilise it with their own students.

3) Taking Online Courses and Attending Webinars

There are numerous online courses and webinars available that allow educators to learn at their own pace and on their own schedule. These may range from classroom management and instructional design to subject-specific knowledge.

Do you deliver webinars? Take a look at our brand new Webinar Accreditation service!

4) Participating in Peer-to-Peer Learning

Educators can learn from their peers through various activities, such as lesson observations, peer coaching, and collaborative planning. This can provide educators with valuable feedback and insights into their teaching practises, allowing them to develop new strategies and approaches.

5) Self-Directed Learning

Educators can participate in self-directed learning by reading professional literature, conducting research, and reflecting on their teaching practise. This can assist them in identifying areas for improvement as well as developing new skills and knowledge.

### Why is CPD important?

1) Up to Date Teaching Practise

CPD allows educators to learn about new teaching methods, technologies, and research findings, which improves their teaching practise. Educators can improve their teaching practise and better meet the needs of their students by expanding their knowledge and skills.

2) Better Career Performance

Participating in CPD activities can assist educators in developing new skills, gaining new insights, and staying current with the latest trends and developments in their field. This can lead to increased job satisfaction and opportunities for advancement.

3) Improve Student Outcomes

Educators who participate in CPD activities are better prepared to meet the diverse and ever-changing needs of their students. This can result in better student outcomes, such as increased engagement and higher academic achievement, and happier students!

4) Collect CPD Credits or Points

CPD may be required for educators to maintain their professional status through the collection of CPD points or credits. Many teachers are required to undertake a certain amount of hours engaged in CPD learning to ensure they are meeting the highest professional development standards and are committed to continuous learning and growth. Learn more about CPD credits here!

5) Encourage Lifelong Learning

Engaging in regular CPD is a great way to adopt the mindset of lifelong learning and continuous improvement, an attitude that is crucial to have in an ever-changing educational landscape.

### How can I choose the best CPD for me?

Now you know why CPD is important - but how do you go about choosing the best CPD course for you?

There are a variety of factors that will contribute to this, such as the style of learning you prefer, whether you want to learn in person or online, how advanced you are in your current role and more. It's also important to make sure the training you choose to partake in is high quality. You can do this by ensuring it's CPD accredited by an accreditation body like The CPD Group.
