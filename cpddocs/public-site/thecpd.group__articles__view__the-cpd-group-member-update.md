#
 The CPD Group: Member Update

 Jess Hart

 September 14, 2023

 3 min read

 Share:

Through our Member Report mechanism and subsequent investigation, we have withdrawn the accreditation for the following organisation:

Organisation Name: LA Beauty Dolls Limited

Approved CPD Provider Number: 779378

Reason for CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal: breach of The CPD Group's Terms and Conditions, in particular: 7.2.4 & 7.2.5

CPD Accreditation Service Withdrawal Date: 7th of September 2023

### Background:

The above organisation gained Approved CPD Provider status from The CPD Group on the 28th of September 2021.

Listed below are the CPD Training courses that LA Beauty Dolls Limited had CPD Accredited by us and the Accreditation terms associated to each subject title:

·      Safety in Medicine #1010595 – 5 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 11 March 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Foundation Anti-Wrinkle #1010603 – 12 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 14 March 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      First Aid Anaphylaxis & CPR #1010653 - 5 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 21 March 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Anatomy & Physiology Level 4 #1010740 – 9 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 25 March 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Cellulite Removal Workshop # 1010887 – 4 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 08 April 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Advanced Dermal Filler Workshop #1010899 – 3 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 08 April 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Foundation Dermal Filler #1011072 – 5 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 04 May 2022 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Microneedling #1012748 – 9 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 16 February 2023 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Skin Boosters #1012749 – 4 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 16 February 2023 – Expires 07 September 2023)

·      Fat Dissolving #1014295 – 9 Credits - Accreditation Term (Accredited: 14 August 2023 – Expires 07 September 2023)

The above listed subjects are the ONLY CPD Training titles and subjects that have been CPD Accredited by The CPD Group. If any member of the public is in receipt of certification from LA Beauty Dolls Limited that presents one of the above listed titles or subjects, and is dated within the Accreditation Term, your certification holds valid CPD Accreditation and is not affected by this withdrawal of services.

However, if any member of the public are in receipt of certification from LA Beauty Dolls Limited that presents an alternative course title or subject, or it is dated outside of the Accreditation Term listed above, you can be assured it has not been granted CPD Accreditation by The CPD Group. However, please be advised that this does not mean that the course was not Accredited as LA Beauty Dolls Limited may have the specific course title or subject CPD Accredited by an alternative CPD Accreditation Body.  We would highly recommend contacting the Training Provider directly with your enquires.

The CPD Group take all comments, feedback, complaints, concerns, queries good, bad or indifferent extremely seriously in order to ensure high standards of CPD and will fully investigate all reports thoroughly.
