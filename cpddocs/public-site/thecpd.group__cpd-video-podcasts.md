## What is Video Podcast Accreditation?

 Validate your video podcast's educational value. Our accreditation process
 assesses its quality and alignment with CPD standards, boosting your credibility
 and attracting learners.

 Getting your Video Podcasts accredited with us is simple, here's how it works in 4 steps:

### Provider

 Before you accredit your Video Podcast with us, you'll need to become a
 CPD
 Provider
 so we know you're CPD ready.

### Delivery

 We'll assess the expertise of the person delivering the Video Podcast, ensuring they
 are qualified to do so.

### Content

 We'll need to see a detailed summary of the contents of the Video Podcast to ensure
 quality learning.

### QR Code

 We'll then provide you with a QR Code to display at your Video Podcast for learners
 to get their CPD certificate.
