## What the assessors keep asking for (summary of the feedback)

- One subject per application. "Pathway" or combined courses are refused at pre-screening (Biab & Gel polish overlays, 27 May 2026).
- Title includes "CPD Course" so it does not read as a regulated qualification (contract 3.1.7; Gel Polish feedback). Our two newest applications already do this.
- The Description field must be a real description: intro, what is covered, learning outcomes, entry requirements. A website link alone fails "Details". It is copied to The CPD Register once accredited.
- Prerequisites in the Description too; they recommend a minimum Level 2 Beauty (or equivalent) for nails.
- Manual: around 40–50 pages for a nails subject, with anatomically correct images, diagrams, legislation, health and safety; broken-up text, images in anatomy and physiology, contraindications, nail conditions, equipment and treatment protocol sections. The "6 Simple Steps" guide gives the expected chapter order.
- Separate theory and practical assessments with written criteria for judging the practical (their marking-sheet template is resource 55).
- A lesson plan or agenda whose hours match the duration on the application.
- Author CV or overview of expertise as well as certificates (CV template is resource 49).
- Duration: 1 hour of study = 1 CPD credit, including home study and case studies.
- Accessibility statement in the Additional Information field (this one has passed).
