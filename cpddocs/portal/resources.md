- 46 **6 Simple Steps to Writing and Setting out Your Learning Materials for Beauty and Aesthetic Treatments** (PDF). The beauty-specific manual structure:
  1. Introduction: about the trainer and business; learning outcomes; what the course covers; practical demonstration and models; how to declare learning requirements; prerequisites.
  2. Health and safety / legislation: summarise each relevant law briefly (only ones relevant to the treatment, UK and current); hygiene, sterilisation and sanitation for the treatment, lone and mobile working, ergonomics, code of conduct and professional appearance.
  3. Anatomy and physiology: only what is relevant to the treatment; build on what a Level 2 learner already knows.
  4. Treatment information: background and origin; consultation and managing expectations with an example consultation form; relevant images, especially for techniques; treatment planning, pricing and promotion ideas.
  5. Assessment criteria: how candidates are assessed (practical demonstrations, quizzes; include the questions); what is required for certification; practise on each other, then models.
  6. Conclusion: recap the outcomes, invite questions and feedback, next steps and further training, ongoing support.
  Plus PowerPoint rules (six words and one image per slide, one message per slide, dark background, no red or yellow text, sans-serif, never paste the manual into slides) and the agenda-versus-lesson-plan note above.
