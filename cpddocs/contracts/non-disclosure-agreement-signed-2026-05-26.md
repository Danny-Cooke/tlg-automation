# The CPD Group: Non-Disclosure Agreement (signed)

Source: portal.thecpd.group/my-account/documents → Open. Signed by Jessica 2026-05-26 14:11:26. Document last modified 2025-09-30. Captured 2026-10-26.
This is the NDA The CPD Group gives *us* (the Disclosing Party): they, as Receiving Party, promise to keep our manuals confidential and only use them to assess accreditation.

## Recitals

(1) The Disclosing Party wishes to disclose to the Receiving Party (The CPD Group) information which is confidential to the Disclosing Party. Such disclosure to be only for the Stated Purposes of Reviewing and Accrediting CPD Activities for and on behalf of the Disclosing Party.
(2) The Disclosing Party wishes to ensure that that information is kept confidential and to prevent the Receiving Party from misusing or further disclosing that information to third parties without authorisation.
(3) In consideration of the Disclosing Party disclosing the Confidential Information to the Receiving Party, the Receiving Party hereby agrees that it will accept the Confidential Information subject to, and in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 "Confidential Information" means the information outlined in Schedule 1 and any further information designated at or before the time of disclosure by the Disclosing Party as confidential, in whatever tangible or intangible form. "Intellectual Property Rights" means (a) any and all rights subsisting in any jurisdiction in any patents, trade marks, service marks, registered designs, applications, trade, business and company names, internet domain names and email addresses, unregistered trade marks and service marks, copyrights, database rights, know-how, trade and other secrets, rights in designs and inventions; (b) rights under licences, consents, orders, statutes or otherwise in relation to a right in (a); (c) rights of the same or similar effect; (d) the right to sue for past infringements. "Stated Purposes" means the purposes set out in Schedule 2.
1.2 "writing" includes electronic communication; statutes as amended; "this Agreement" as amended; Schedules, Clauses, Parties as usual.
1.3 Headings for convenience only. 1.4 Singular includes plural.

## 2. Disclosure

2.1 The Disclosing Party has a commercial or other interest in all such Confidential Information.
2.2 The Receiving Party shall, subject to Clause 7, at all times maintain as confidential and shall not use, exploit or reproduce any part of the Confidential Information for any purposes other than the Stated Purposes without express written consent. Unauthorised purposes include: 2.2.1 reproducing any part of it, or investigating undisclosed aspects; 2.2.2 using it to procure commercial advantage for the Receiving Party or disadvantage to the Disclosing Party; 2.2.3 carrying out processes, making inventions, further developments or applications for registered IP rights based on it.
2.3 Reasonable organisational, physical and technical measures to preserve confidentiality.
2.4 Personal data (Data Protection Act 2018): the Receiving Party complies with the Act, and binds third parties under Clause 5 to the same.
2.5 Following fulfilment of the Stated Purposes or on demand, the Receiving Party returns all Confidential Information and certifies that no copies have been retained.

## 3. Storage of Confidential Information

3.1 Stored only in a designated dual-access, secure cloud-based storage folder. 3.2 Hard copies in a locked cabinet when not in use. 3.3 Electronic copies exclusively on the Receiving Party's private internal servers, access restricted to authorised personnel, no access from external networks or third-party platforms. 3.4 The Disclosing Party may request reasonable changes to storage arrangements.

## 4. Employee Undertakings

4.1 The Receiving Party obtains enforceable undertakings from all employees with access. 4.2 No disclosure until those undertakings are provided.

## 5. Third Party Disclosure and Undertakings

5.1 No disclosure to a professional adviser or other third party without (5.1.1) express written consent of the Disclosing Party and (5.1.2) an enforceable undertaking from the third party. 5.2 Consent not granted until the undertaking is received.

## 6. Proprietary Rights

The Confidential Information and all IP rights remain the property of the Disclosing Party; disclosure confers no rights on the Receiving Party.

## 7. Exceptions

Obligations do not apply to information that: 7.1 is already known to the Receiving Party free of confidentiality; 7.2 is or comes into the public domain other than through breach; 7.3 is received from a third party free of confidentiality; 7.4 was independently developed beforehand; 7.5 is disclosed by the Disclosing Party to a third party free of confidentiality; 7.6 is approved for disclosure in writing; 7.7 is declared no longer confidential; 7.8 must be disclosed under the Freedom of Information Act 2000; 7.9 is required by law, court or government agency, with advance notice.

## 8. Term

8.1 Confidentiality continues indefinitely, subject to Clause 7. 8.2 On expiry or termination, return the Confidential Information and retain no copies.

## 9. Enforcement and Indemnity

9.1 Damages alone unlikely to be adequate. 9.2 The Disclosing Party is entitled to injunction, specific performance and other equitable relief. 9.3 The Receiving Party indemnifies the Disclosing Party against all liabilities, loss, damage, costs and expenses (including legal costs on a full indemnity basis) arising from its breach, including breaches of the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 10. Exclusion and Limitation of Liability

10.1 No obligation on the Disclosing Party to enter further transactions or provide particular information. 10.2 No duty of care or warranty by the Disclosing Party that the Confidential Information is accurate or complete. 10.3 Nothing limits liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation (including copyright infringement).

## Schedule 1: Protected Information

- Copyrighted materials: training manuals, guides, handouts, workbooks, PowerPoint presentations, slides and visual content, video/audio recordings of training sessions, assessments, tests, case studies, digital course materials (e-learning modules, online course content).
- Trade secrets & know-how: unique training methodologies or frameworks, proprietary processes/models/approaches to teaching, instructional techniques or exercises, internal strategies on delivery, structure or assessment.
- Branding & design elements: logos, distinctive branding, custom illustrations, diagrams, infographics.
- Data & supporting materials: research, statistics, proprietary data, client case studies or anonymised examples.

## Schedule 2: Stated Purposes

- To review, assess and evaluate the training materials, learning outcomes, delivery methods and supporting documentation submitted, to determine whether the training meets The CPD Group's accreditation framework.
- To enable internal staff and duly appointed assessors to conduct quality assurance checks, compliance reviews and related accreditation activities.
- To generate reports, feedback or recommendations to the Disclosing Party.
- To maintain records of accredited courses for regulatory, auditing and ongoing quality assurance purposes.
- The Receiving Party shall not use the Confidential Information for the delivery of training, the creation of derivative works, or any commercial purpose other than administering the accreditation services.
