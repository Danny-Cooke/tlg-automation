# cpd-tlg: our CPD course materials

One subfolder per course. Each has `submit-to-cpd/` (the files to upload to The CPD Group portal, nothing else), `working-files/` (originals, PDFs, figure sources, critiques, fix logs) and a checklist for Jess.

- `builder-gel-course/`: Builder Gel Overlay and Short Extension CPD Course (#22521). Manual and B02–B06 pack at v1.4 (8 Nov 2026: Jess's pictures replace Figures 1, 14, 15, 19, 20 and 21, a finished-set photo sits in Chapter 2 and the EL Nail Systems product shot closes Chapter 8; `working-files/build/apply_photos_2026-11-08.py` then `swap_figures_2026-11-08.py`) ready for Jess's review; see `JESS - what to do in each file.docx`.
- `gel-polish-course/`: Gel Polish Nails CPD Course (#21252, Action Required). Manual v2.0 written from scratch on 2026-10-26 to the Builder Gel standard, with the G02–G06 pack, ten original figures (`working-files/build/` holds the scripts) and `JESS - what to do in each file.docx`, which includes the paste-ready application text. v2.1 (2026-11-02) puts Jess's own pictures in for seven of the technique figures, a finished set in Chapter 2 and the kit list after Chapter 8; G06 went to v2.1 and G07 (reflective practice form, on The CPD Group template) was added. v2.2 (2026-11-05) answers the assessor's recommendation on v2.1: Figure 1 re-lettered so it reads in print (`working-files/build/fig01_relabel_2026-11-05.py`), Figures 18, 20 to 22 replaced with Jess's 4 November pictures, G06 v2.2. Build notes in `working-files/G01-build-notes-2026-10-26.md`.
