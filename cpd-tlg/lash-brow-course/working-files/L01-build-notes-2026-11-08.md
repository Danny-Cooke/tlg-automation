# Lash and Brow Tint and Wax CPD Course: how v1.0 was built

Built 8 November 2026 from scratch, to the standard of the Gel Polish course (`../gel-polish-course`) and The CPD Group's "6 Simple Steps" structure (`cpddocs/portal-resources/46`), at Jess's request: "make this manual look and feel like a CPD course too, following syllabus". No application has been opened yet; the fifth slot on the provider contract is also claimed by the Electric File course, so one of the two needs a new slot (see the checklist).

## The source material and why it was not reused

`source/The looking Glass - Lash & Brow Tint and Wax Beauty Course.pdf` is the school's existing 55-page manual (a plain-text copy is beside it). It was read for scope: lash tint, brow tint, brow wax, tweezing, trimming, the kit list, the five brow shapes, brow measuring, the patch test, aftercare. Its text is not reused:

- Forty of its pages are generic salon material (legislation, sterilisation, insurance, skin anatomy, a hair-removal methods table, a general-health contraindications table about electrical treatments and massage) with no lash or brow content; the treatment procedures occupy three pages.
- The legislation was out of date or wrong: "Electricity at Work Regulations 1992" (1989), "RIDDOR 1995" (2013), "PPE 2002" (1992, amended 2022), "Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 2008" (superseded by the retained Regulation 1223/2009), "Disability Discrimination Act 1996" with the Trade Descriptions Act text pasted under it, the Management Regulations listed twice, a defunct HSE phone number, Trade Descriptions and Sale and Supply of Goods Acts in place of the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the DMCC Act 2024.
- Several technical statements were unsafe or outdated: the patch test "at least 24 hours" (manufacturers and insurers commonly require 48; the course sets 48), a lash tint with the client "sitting upright" and the lower lashes done with the eyes open, a "hot/cold, sharp/blunt" sensitivity test (that is the thermal and tactile test for electrical treatments, not a wax test), no mention of the under-16 restriction, no developer strength, no eye-irrigation procedure, "glass bead steriliser", and a hair-growth cycle paragraph that describes telogen under the catagen heading.
- Its images (stock photographs of equipment and conditions, a brow-shapes graphic, an eye-anatomy diagram) are not the school's to reuse and were not copied.

## What the course teaches (the syllabus)

Twenty-one chapters in the Gel Polish order: specification; purpose, benefits and scope (with the related services compared, a short history and pricing without figures); anatomy (skin, hair and follicle, growth cycle, eye and lids, brow and its four muscles); conditions and contraindications (eye, skin, medication and treatment tables; the under-16 rule); consultation and the patch test; how tint works (oxidative chemistry, developer at about 3%, shade and timing, sensitisation, the Annex III legal position); brow design (three mapping lines, five shapes); kit (room, tinting kit, hot and strip wax, tools, COSHH); health, safety and hygiene; legislation, insurance, data and advertising; preparing the client (order: tint, tidy, shape); eyelash tinting; eyebrow tinting (checked at one minute); eyebrow shaping (wax, tweeze, trim); results and troubleshooting; contra-actions and emergencies (eye irrigation); aftercare; conclusion; activities and knowledge checks; forms A to H (Form G is the patch-test record); references.

Deliberate decisions: one application for the three treatments, justified in the manual and the cover note as one treatment group in the occupational standards; patch test at least 48 hours before, every new product family, every six months, every change in health or medication, never for a client who has reacted to hair dye or black henna; no tint under 16 (the products' mandatory warning), parental consent and insurer agreement at 16 and 17; eyes closed from the first pad to the last wipe; brows tinted before they are waxed (Jess's own order in the source); hot (peelable) wax as the default for the eye area with strip wax allowed; the mobile eyelid never waxed; one spatula per dip; no brand or product named; no £ figures.

Seven safety-critical criteria: patch test and age; eyes closed, protected, tint in the eye irrigated; wax temperature tested and no wax on the eyelid; one spatula per dip and single-use items; skin taut, parallel removal, no wax over contraindicated skin; refusal of a red or sore eye and of contraindications; hygiene.

## Documents

- `L01 ... Learner Manual v1.0.docx`: 21 chapters, 13 original figures, 48 tables, about 23 000 words, 77 pages in Word. Built by `build/build_manual.py`, which opens the Gel Polish G01 v2.2 only for its styles, numbering and table formats and writes every paragraph new; validated with the docx skill's validator.
- `build/figures.py` draws Figures 1 to 13 with matplotlib in the nail courses' palette: skin section, hair and follicle, growth cycle, eye and lids, brow parts and muscles, four eye conditions (illustrations, not photographs), the patch test, how tint works, brow mapping, five brow shapes, lash tinting stages, brow waxing, eye irrigation.
- `L02` theory paper (28 MCQ + 8 short + 2 scenarios, 50 marks, pass 40), `L03` mark scheme, `L04` practical sheet (20 criteria, 7 SC), `L05` lesson plan (15 hours: 1.5 + 7.5 + 6; learners tint each other in the morning, models in the afternoon; models patch tested at the school at least 48 hours before), `L06` cover note (new application), `L07` reflective practice form (CPD template 64): `build/build_pack.py` and `build/make_reflective_form.py`.
- `JESS - what to do in each file.md/.docx` with the paste-ready application text; `build/make_checklist.py`.
- Website: `academy/content/courses.json` has a `lash-brow` entry, `website/academy/pages/lash-and-brow-tint-and-wax-cpd-course.html` is generated by `website/academy/course-pages.py`, and the card is in `website/academy/courses-section.html` (the intro now says "nail and beauty courses"). Publishing needs the WordPress login.
- PDF exports with Word COM for checking are in this folder; upload the .docx files.
