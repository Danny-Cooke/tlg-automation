# Critique: B01 Builder Gel Learner Manual v1 against The CPD Group's ACCREDITED framework

Reviewed 2026-10-26 against the assessor feedback on #21252 and #20892, the "6 Simple Steps" guide, the lesson plan template, the published framework and contract clause 3.1. The manual is 15,500 words, 20 chapters, 43 tables, 14 placed images (10 credited clinical photographs, 3 original diagrams; one further diagram is embedded but never placed).

## Verdict

Not a clean pass as submitted. Expect it to come back once, for Details and probably Tested. It is a different class of document from the Gel Polish manual and should pass on the second round if the points below are fixed. Everything the assessors asked for on the earlier courses is structurally present: one subject, "CPD Course" in the title, Level 2 prerequisite, 40+ pages, UK legislation and health and safety, anatomy with real photographs, separate theory and practical assessment, three case studies, author section, duration broken down, aftercare, references.

## Will fail: Details (D)

Gel Polish manual text was reused and not fully converted. An assessor who read the Gel Polish manual in August will spot it. Lines refer to the text extract B01_manual_text.txt.

1. Line 350, heat-spike prevention table: "do not place a large bead of structural gel in this gel-polish service". Direct contradiction of Chapter 12 step 2 ("Place a bead of builder gel").
2. Figure 14 (Chapter 7, the apex diagram): the image itself is titled "Figure 3", its header says "Gel-polish scope is the top panel only", the builder-overlay panel says "Separate builder-gel training required", the longer-enhancement panel says "Outside this course". The caption beneath says the opposite. Redraw for this course.
3. Figure 13 (Chapter 6): the image is titled "Figure 2. Photopolymerisation" while the caption says Figure 13. Fix the numbering in the image.
4. Line 668, ASA table: "'Completed the Gel Nail Polish CPD Course'". Wrong course name.
5. Line 662, ASA table: "Provides a cosmetic colour coating". Builder gel is a structural product.
6. Line 201: "Structural correction is outside this gel-polish course". In a builder gel course it is in scope.
7. Line 217, Figure 2 caption: "Do not apply or reapply gel polish".
8. Lines 180 and 195: "modified gel-polish service", "Do not promise that gel polish will cure the cause".
9. Line 293 treatment plan and Form G header: "Named base, colour and top" / "Base/colour/top". Should be slip layer, builder gel, top coat.
10. Line 297: service variations listed as "French finish or nail art" (gel polish). Should be overlay, short extension on tip, on form, infill, rebalance, removal.
11. Line 367: "most often implicated in gel-polish allergy" (minor).
12. Chapter 6 covers the heat spike twice: "Heat spikes" (lines 346–357, copied from the gel polish manual) and "The heat spike" (384–397). Merge them. The framework's Creativity criterion is "non-repetitive".

## Likely to fail: Tested (T)

The manual promises "a written paper totalling 50 marks" and a practical observed against "safety-critical criteria", but neither the paper nor the criteria are in the manual, and the activity log for #22521 shows only the manual and nine certificate screenshots were uploaded. On Gel Polish the assessor wrote: "We require both a theory assessment and a practical (hands-on) assessment, with clear criteria for how the practical work will be judged." Upload the theory paper with its mark scheme and the practical marking sheet (their template is cpddocs/portal-resources/55). Chapter 18's twelve knowledge checks are unmarked and are not the paper.

## At risk: Content and Creativity (C, C)

- Chapters 11 to 14, the actual technique (prep, slip layer and apex, tips and forms, infill and removal), contain no images at all. The assessor's complaint on Gel Polish was text-heavy sections and no images in "equipment and treatment protocol sections"; the "6 Simple Steps" guide says "include relevant images, especially if there are specific techniques". Add step photographs or diagrams: bead placement, apex from the side, barrel view of the C-curve, tip fitting, form fitting, the hairline gap.
- Chapter 8 (kit) has no images either.
- Figure 4, the nail-shapes diagram, is embedded in the file but not placed anywhere; it is also still labelled for gel polish ("safest gel-polish choice", "almond, coffin and stiletto normally require an extension system and separate training"). Either delete it from the file or redraw and place it in Chapter 7.

## Missing from the submission (not the manual)

- Lesson plan or agenda whose hours match the application. The "6 Simple Steps" guide: "It is a good idea to submit one or the other... so the assessors can... see if the hours stated match the hours on the application." The manual says 17.5 hours total (2.5 pre-course, 7.5 taught, 7.5 case studies); check the application says 17.5, not 15.
- Author CV. The Gel Polish assessor asked for it explicitly; only screenshots went up with #22521.
- A conclusion chapter. The guide wants a recap of the outcomes, an invitation for questions and feedback, and what the learner can do next. The manual stops at forms and references.
- The application's Description field: it must carry the intro, content summary, outcomes and the Level 2 prerequisite. If it was filled the way Gel Polish was (a web link), it fails Details and Eligibility regardless of the manual. Not checkable from the portal while the course is in review.

## Verify before resubmitting (Content accuracy)

The manual cites specific instruments that should be checked against legislation.gov.uk before an assessor does: SI 2026/23 and SI 2026/109 (TPO in Great Britain, 15 August 2026 and 14 February 2027 dates) and the Frontiers in Immunology 2026 paper (doi 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1941127) credited for Figure 5. The EU TPO ban from 1 September 2025 and Regulation (EU) 2020/1682 are correct. If any citation does not resolve, remove it; a wrong legal citation in a manual that leans on legal precision would be an easy Content fail.

## Smaller points

- "Most educators teach acrylic application in three zones" (Chapter 7) reads as copied from the Acrylic manual; say builder gel.
- Treatment information per the guide should include origin or background of the treatment and pricing or promotion ideas; Chapter 2 has neither. Low risk.
- Tell learners explicitly that they must bring a model for the practical assessment. Low risk.

## What is good and should stay

Real clinical photographs with licences credited; the original nail-unit diagram is anatomically sound; the BIAB trademark note; the four-step observe-ask-decide-record rule; the removal-category table; the legislation table with UK nations; the forms with data-protection fields; dated references. These are the things the Gel Polish manual lacked.
