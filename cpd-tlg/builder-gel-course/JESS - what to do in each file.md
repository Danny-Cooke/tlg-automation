- Activity Name: Builder Gel Overlay and Short Extension CPD Course
- Brief Description (paste, 997 characters, under the 1,000 limit): A one-day classroom CPD course for qualified beauty and nail professionals who want to add builder gel overlays and short extensions to their services. You will revisit nail anatomy and the conditions that mean a service must be adapted or refused, understand how gel cures and why builder gel carries a heat-spike risk, and learn to prepare the natural nail, apply a slip layer, build a balanced overlay with a correctly placed apex, create a short extension on a tip or form, and carry out infills and safe removal. Also covered: allergy prevention, UK legislation, consultation, aftercare and honest advertising. Assessed by a written theory paper, an observed practical on a model and three post-course case studies. Entry requirements: aged 18 or over, with a Level 2 Beauty Therapy, Nail Services or equivalent qualification covering manicure, nail structure and safe salon practice; prior gel polish experience strongly recommended. 17.5 hours including pre-course reading and case studies.
- Duration (hours): 17.5
- How is your Activity Delivered: tick Classroom only.
- Learning Outcomes (paste): Upon successful completion learners will be able to: LO1 explain the purpose, benefits, limitations and client suitability of builder gel, and compare it with gel polish, hard gel and liquid-and-powder acrylic; LO2 identify the structures of the nail unit and recognise conditions that require the service to be adapted, postponed or refused; LO3 conduct and record a professional consultation, obtain informed consent and create an individual treatment plan; LO4 explain oligomers, monomers, photoinitiators, polymerisation, lamp compatibility, under-curing and the inhibition layer; LO5 explain why builder gel carries a heat-spike risk that gel polish does not, and control it; LO6 select, handle, store and dispose of products and equipment in accordance with risk assessment, COSHH, manufacturer instructions and current UK requirements; LO7 prepare the natural nail without avoidable damage to the plate or living tissue; LO8 apply a slip layer and build a balanced overlay with a correctly placed apex, straight sidewalls and an even C-curve; LO9 create a short extension on a tip or form within the product's stated capability; LO10 infill, rebalance and remove builder gel safely by the method the product requires, and provide appropriate aftercare. Learners benefit by adding a higher-value, longer-wearing structural service to their menu, delivered safely within their insurance and the law.
